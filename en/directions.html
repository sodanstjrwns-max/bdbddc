From Camp Humphreys & Osan Air Base

For U.S. military members and families stationed in the Pyeongtaek area — Seoul BD Dental in Cheonan is an easy drive south, with English support, transparent published pricing, and itemized English receipts for overseas insurance claims (e.g., TRICARE Overseas Program).

From Camp Humphreys (Anjeong-ri gate) About 35–40 minutes / approx. 40 km. Take Route 38 or the Seohaean Expressway toward Cheonan, exit at Buldang-dong. Free parking on site.
From Osan Air Base (Songtan) About 45–50 minutes by car via Route 1 (Gyeongbu) southbound to Cheonan. Or take Line 1 subway to Cheonan-Asan / Cheonan Station, then a 10–15 minute taxi.
By Train (no car needed) SRT/KTX from Pyeongtaek Jije to Cheonan-Asan Station takes about 10 minutes, then a short taxi ride to the clinic. Show the driver: “충남 천안시 서북구 불당34길 14”
