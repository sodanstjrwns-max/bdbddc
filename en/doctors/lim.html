Director Ji-won Lim’s story

Orthodontic care with precise treatment and attentive communication

What led me to become a dentist

Since I was young, I subjects with clear answers suited me well. Math and physics, where principles are clear and results can be explained with reasons and logic, suited me well.

Interestingly, orthodontics is the area of healthcare where physical concepts are applied most deeply. The fact that it actually “moves” parts of the body to create change was a major draw for me.

Also Adolescence—full of dreams and pure energy—Because I’ve always loved that stage, caring for those kids’ teeth and growth through orthodontics was a natural choice for me.

✦

Clinical Expertise

Within orthodontics, I focus on non-surgical camouflage treatment for Class 3 malocclusion—an area in which I have extensive experience and confidence.

Many cases lie on the borderline between surgical and non-surgical approaches; in such situations, meticulous analysis and clear direction determine success.

When I untangle complex factors one by one and achieve satisfying results without surgery, I’m reminded of the essential appeal of orthodontics.

✦

Treatment Style

If I were to sum up my treatment style in one sentence

"Proper care and attentive communication"

Orthodontic treatment is a long journey—sometimes a few months, sometimes 2~3 years. That’s why I make it a rule to explain fully what we’re doing now and why each step is needed. When we share information and understand the direction together, the process becomes much more comfortable.

✦

Treatment Philosophy

What I consider most important in orthodontic care is

accurate diagnosis and treatment planning

There is a right time for every tooth movement. Finding the 'optimal treatment timing'—that is the crux of orthodontic care. This is exactly why I invest ample time in analysis and planning before treatment.

✦

Advice for Patients

Oral hygiene during orthodontic treatment cannot be overemphasized. Plaque tends to build up around appliances, so proper brushing and hygiene significantly affect the quality of treatment.

"Orthodontics is not just about straightening teeth; it’s a time to build habits that keep them healthy."
✦

A Memorable Case

A patient with a severe open bite who had been advised to undergo surgery at another cliniccomes to mind. After consultation, I determined that non-surgical treatment could sufficiently improve the case, so we began. After a long period of working together, we achieved a satisfying result.

After treatment, the patient beamed and said "I can’t believe we achieved this without surgery—thank you so much."I felt deeply rewarded as an orthodontist.

Clinic Director Interview Q&A

Dr. Jiwon Im answers frequently asked questions

When is non-surgical orthodontic treatment possible?

Cases on the borderline between surgical and non-surgical options require precise analysis. For Class III malocclusion, we determine the feasibility of non-surgical treatment by comprehensively evaluating the degree of skeletal discrepancy and the limits of tooth movement. During your consultation, I will provide thorough explanations and propose a thoughtful, tailored treatment option.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Because each person’s teeth and the amount of movement required differ, I can give you an accurate timeframe after a consultation. Before starting, I invest ample time in precise planning so treatment proceeds efficiently without unnecessary extension.

Can I get Invisalign treatment?

Yes. I continuously study Invisalign clear aligner therapy and apply it across a range of cases. Depending on your situation, we will discuss and decide together whether braces or clear aligners are more suitable.

Any message for patients?

Orthodontic treatment is a journey that takes time, but the result at the end is certainly worth it. If you have any questions, please feel free to come in for a consultation anytime. With thorough explanations, we will find the personalized treatment plan that suits you best.
