Bringing the smile hidden behind your hand out into the world

What inspired me to become a dentist

As a child, I watched friends hide their smiles because they felt insecure about their teeth. I realized that a single tooth can change your expression—and when your expression changes, your entire confidence can change too.

Since then, I came to believe a dentist isn’t merely a technician who treats what hurts, a profession that turns the light back on in a person’s life—that’s what I came to believe.

In front of the mirror, when a patient regains the bright smile they had lost and says "Now I can smile with ease"— those moving moments are what led me to where I am today.

✦

Areas of Expertise

I especially focus on esthetic care for the front teeth—Glownate (Laminate Veneer), esthetic composite resin, restoring chipped front teeth and closing gaps—and I have a deep affection for these areas.

The moment a yellowed tooth regains a clear, glossy luster, the moment a long-held complex disappears and the face brightens, the moment a broken tooth’s natural shape is reborn— I feel less like a dentist and more like a sculptorat heart.

I don’t stop at restoring function; 'their own natural beauty'—and giving it back is what I’m most proud of.
✦

My Treatment Style

My treatment style can be summed up in one sentence.

"Stitch by stitch, uncompromising meticulousness."

To be honest, I don’t work fast. But I value 'a high-quality result'more. I work with relentless attention to detail so that what I treat won’t need to be redone.

For me, this slowness is a form of devotion—and responsibility—to my patients.

✦

My Treatment Philosophy

There is one principle I consider most important in my care.

"Put myself in your shoes—if I were the patient, if this were my family."

I understand better than anyone the fear of lying back with your mouth open in the dental chair. So I keep asking myself: 'If I were the one in this chair, which treatment would feel most comfortable?' 'What is the most appropriate option I could recommend to my own family?'

Since the day I first put on my white coat, this has been an unwavering principle for me.

✦

Advice I Give My Patients

The advice I often share is simple—but it’s the most powerful.

"Brushing alone isn’t enough. Flossing and regular checkups—never compromise on these two."

A toothbrush can’t reach the tiny spaces between teeth. Using floss once a day isn’t optional—it’s essential, and a checkup every 6 months is effective prevention that can spare you minor pain and major costs.

Prevention is always the best treatment.

✦

Memorable Patients

It’s hard to choose just one memorable patient. To me, every treatment is like a story.

After treatment, looking in the mirror "I can smile without covering my mouth now"said a middle-aged gentleman, and after we closed the space between his front teeth, "I can smile confidently even on stage"and a young man who shared his joy…

Those moments when they shake off their insecurities and regain their confidence— those smiles call me back to the clinic every day.

✦

Current focus

Lately, I’ve been immersed in Seoul BD Dental’s signature Laminate Veneer brand 'Glownate'—and I am deeply focused on no-prep, conservative esthetic composite resin.

Without unnecessarily shaving down teeth worn with age, Glownate rebuilds only what’s lost with ceramic. Even front teeth chipped from wear can be restored to their original shape with no-prep Laminate Veneers. With DSD (Digital Smile Design), you can preview the result before treatment and design your most beautiful smile together with us.

Preserving your natural teeth while bringing back the healthy contours of your youth— that is the essence of the esthetic composite resin work I pursue.
✦

A personal note

My colleagues describe me as "a perfectionist—almost to a fault—in the operatory, and a planner in everything else"they say. The sensitivity that won’t allow even small errors may well be a tiring trait. But when I’m caring for your teeth, I have no intention of setting it aside.

And surprisingly, outside the clinic I’m someone who’s completely disarmed by cute things. Seeing round, soft characters like Totoro, Moomin, or Doraemon puts me at ease, and a day full of edgy focus gently unwinds. You could call it my ‘unexpected side.’ 😊

I hope Seoul BD Dental will be, for our patients,
"not a scary place, but a reassuring one"I hope it remains that way.

A place where the tension you feel coming in turns into relief as you leave,
where a delicate touch minimizes discomfort, and one treatment leads to results that last.
Becoming that kind of lifelong dentist for you is my goal.

Director Interview

A message from Head Director Jeongmin Hyun

Is there a reason you focus especially on Glownate (Laminate Veneers)?

A single tooth can change a person’s expression, and when the expression changes, confidence follows. Glownate is Seoul BD Dental’s distinctive Laminate Veneer brand that creates natural, beautiful results while minimizing tooth reduction. When I see patients in front of the mirror saying, "Now I can smile with peace of mind," it reinforces my conviction to keep advancing Glownate.

How is Glownate different from conventional Laminate Veneers?

At Seoul BD Dental, Glownate is our Laminate Veneer planned with DSD (Digital Smile Design) to suit your face shape, skin tone, and gum line, then performed with minimal or no-prep reduction. Unlike conventional veneers that often remove more tooth structure, we rebuild only what’s been worn with ceramic, preserving your natural teeth as much as possible. Natural, tooth-like beauty—that’s the core of Glownate.

Any message you’d especially like to share with patients?

Please commit to just two things: floss daily and come in for a checkup every 6 months. The tiny spaces between teeth that a toothbrush can’t reach can turn into big problems. Prevention is always the best treatment.
