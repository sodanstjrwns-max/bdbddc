Dr. Minkyu Kim's story

A calm, meticulous Orthodontic Specialist

Why I became a dentist

As a child, while being treated at the dentist, I realized one thing.

"One good treatment can greatly change everyday comfort."

It struck me that a single treatment can not only relieve pain, but also change a person's expression and the feel of their entire day. Over time, that feeling settled into one wish.

"I want to be someone who makes another person's everyday life a little more comfortable."
That desire naturally led me to dentistry, and even now, the resolution I felt then remains at the center of my care.

✦

Clinical Expertise

As an orthodontist, I currently treat a wide range of orthodontic cases, and lately I’ve been focusing on Invisalign-centered digital orthodontics.

More patients are choosing comfortable care with nearly invisible appliances, and advances in technology keep expanding the types of cases we can treat. Because the appliances are discreet and usually cause less soreness, many patients feel more at ease and are satisfied with their results.

✦

Treatment Style

If I had to describe my approach in one sentence,

"Care that finds the most suitable, comfortable solution for each patient—grounded in scientific evidence."

Orthodontics isn’t just about making teeth look straight; it’s a comprehensive treatment that brings teeth, bone, and muscles into balance. That’s why I always conduct a precise, data-driven analysisand build the most practical treatment plan from it. At the same time, I believe treatment only has meaning when the patient fully understands and agrees.

✦

Treatment Philosophy

The principles I value most are

an accurate diagnosis, a predictable plan, and care the patient understands.

Because orthodontic care can span several years, the plan made at the start has a major impact on the outcome. When the plan is clear, the process stays steady, and patients can naturally understand the course of change.

✦

Advice for Patients

When I explain how to manage your treatment, I always say:

"Orthodontic success isn’t created by the doctor alone."

With Invisalign in particular, how consistently you wear your aligners determines both the pace and the quality of results. Because they’re comfortable, it’s easy to forget them, but a difference of just a few hours per day can translate into months overall. That’s why at each visit I emphasize "we build this treatment together".

✦

A Memorable Experience

One memorable experience was a message a patient sent after completing orthodontic treatment.

As their teeth became straighter, not only did their appearance change, their expression brightened and even their speech grew more confident.

I had thought I was changing only teeth, but hearing that their day-to-day life, relationships, and overall outlook had changed as wellreminded me once again how meaningful this work is.
✦

Current Interests

Recently, Invisalign First and MA (mandibular advancement) for growing patients, and dentofacial orthopedic treatment, as well as advanced digital diagnostic systems. As the field of orthodontics continues to evolve, I keep learning so I can deliver care that is faster and more comfortable for patients.

✦

A Bit About Me

Colleagues describe me as "calm and meticulous". That attention to detail helps in diagnosis and planning. I believe getting the first step right is the most important part of any treatment.

Outside the clinic, I enjoy exercise and reading, and I make time to keep studying new clinical knowledge. Everyday life with my children is my greatest rest and motivation, and it naturally brings more warmth to the way I care for patients.

When patients think of Seoul BD Dental

"It's more comfortable than I expected."

We hope that thought comes to you naturally.
Precise treatment, thorough explanations, and a comfortable atmosphere.
By honoring these three, we want to walk with you through your orthodontic journey—steadily and confidently.

Director Interview

Director Dr. Minkyu Kim shares his story

What led you to become a dentist?

As a child receiving dental care, I realized that "one good treatment can make everyday life far more comfortable." I was struck by how a single treatment can not only reduce pain but also change your expression and the tone of your entire day. That experience led me to become a dentist.

What do you consider most important in orthodontic care?

An accurate diagnosis, a predictable plan, and treatment you can understand. Because orthodontics can be a journey lasting years, the plan at the start heavily influences the outcome. When the plan is clear, the process stays on course, and patients can naturally understand the flow of changes.

Any advice for Invisalign patients?

I often say, "Orthodontics isn’t a result created by the doctor alone." Especially with Invisalign, how faithfully you wear your aligners determines the speed and quality of treatment. A difference of just a few hours a day can add up to months. Please remember: this is a treatment we build together.

What is your most memorable care experience?

A message from a patient after orthodontic treatment. As their teeth aligned, not only did their appearance change, but their expression brightened and their speech became more confident. I thought I had only changed teeth, but hearing that their day, relationships, and the overall tone of life had changed reminded me how meaningful this work is.
