Director Minjin Kim’s Story

A friendly pediatric dentist who meets children at their eye level

Why I became a dentist

I entered dental school from the start with the dream of becoming a pediatric dentist.

When I was young, there was a dentist who treated me with great kindness. Because of that, I came to see the dental office not as a scary place but as “a place with warm, caring adults,” and that memory naturally guided me toward pediatric dentistry.

Now, like that dentist, helping children build positive experiences at the dentistI consider this my mission.

✦

Clinical Expertise

As a pediatric dentist, I provide care ranging from infant and toddler checkups, cavity treatment for children and adolescents, and oral hygiene guidance to growth-phase orthodontic consultations.

Children are not simply small adults; they have age-specific traits, their own pace, and their own emotions.

Even with the same diagnosis, the approach should be completely different. I believe that understanding those differences and designing treatment to fit each child is the true appeal of pediatric dentistry.

✦

Treatment Style

If I were to sum up my treatment style in one sentence

"Approach children at their level with warmth, and when they show courage, praise and encourage them generously."

Children notice the emotional steps before the procedural ones. So when I meet a young patient, I start with simple, gentle words to ease their tension, and I always praise even the smallest act of bravery.

Helping a child reach the moment when they feel "I can do this", I believe, is where real care begins.

✦

Treatment Philosophy

There is one principle I value most in care.

"Children are continually growing, and treatment must match their pace of growth."

Even cavities of the same size call for very different plans depending on age, ability to cooperate, timing of baby-tooth shedding, and stage of growth. So when I make a treatment plan, I consider not only the child’s current status but also their future development as well.

This is not simply treatment; it is also the process of planning a child’s future oral health.

✦

Advice for Parents

I often share two pieces of advice with parents.

First, always floss before bedtime.
Second, reduce the number of snack times (exposure) rather than just the amount.

Many parents don’t realize how much these two habits can change a child’s cavity risk. So at each visit, I explain the reasons clearly and offer practical, personalized guidance each child can follow.

✦

A Moment I Remember Most

My most memorable moments have come from seeing children change.

At first, there was a child so frightened they clung to Mom and could barely get through an exam. But as we built a little rapport at each visit, at some point the child began sitting in the chair on their own and bravely getting through treatment.

On the final day of treatment, that smiling thumbs up has stayed with me. Later, the mother told me, "Even when I try to take my child to another dental clinic, they insist on going only to Seoul BD Dental ." That one comment strengthened my resolve as a pediatric dentist.

✦

Current Interests

Recently, pediatric orthodontics has been my focus of study.

I’m continually exploring what kinds of early interventions can positively influence a child’s future teeth alignment and jaw growth, and how to treat in ways that are safer and less scary.

✦

Personal note

Colleagues describe me as "someone who wonders if there’s a better way and thinks it through once more" they say. I like that. Treating children is always a journey of finding a slightly better way.

Outside of work, taking photos and documenting are my favorites. Capturing children’s rapid growth and everyday moments brings me great joy.

For every child and parent who visits Seoul BD Dental, this is the experience I want to offer:

"A dental office that isn’t scary, and a place you feel okay coming back to."

In this space infused with our Head Directors’ thoughtful care,
I will do my best to help children feel comfortable and build healthy oral habits.

Doctor Interview

A message from Dr. Minjin Kim

Did you always dream of becoming a pediatric dentist?

Yes. Thanks to a kind dentist I met as a child, I came to see the dental office as “a place with warm, caring adults.” That memory led me to pediatric dentistry, and now I see it as my mission to give children a positive experience of the dentist.

What do you consider most important when treating children?

Children read emotions before procedures. So I begin by explaining things gently in simple words to ease their tension, and I always praise even the smallest act of courage. Creating the moment when a child feels, ‘I can do this,’ is where care begins.

Any particular advice for parents?

I emphasize two things. First, always floss before bed. Second, for snacks, reduce the ‘frequency of exposure’ rather than just the amount. These two make a big difference in preventing cavities. At every visit, I explain why and offer practical, individualized guidance for each child.

Is there a moment that stands out most?

At first, a child clung to mom and could only manage a brief checkup. As we built rapport over visits, the child began sitting in the chair independently and bravely received treatment. I still remember the big thumbs-up and smile on the final day. When the mother said, “My child insists on going only to Seoul BD Dental,” my path as a pediatric dentist felt even more solid.
