Dental Implant Center Treatment Areas

6 independent surgical suites · Air-Shower infection control

Standard Dental Implant

Replaces missing teeth with artificial roots. Function and esthetics similar to natural teeth.

Bone Graft (GBR) with Dental Implant

For insufficient bone, implant placement with simultaneous bone grafting.

Full-Mouth Implant

Full-mouth reconstruction such as All-on-4/6 when all teeth are missing.

Sinus Lift

If the posterior upper jaw lacks bone, a Sinus Lift is performed before Dental Implant placement.

6 independent operating rooms

Six independent operating rooms at Seoul National University Hospital level, with an air-shower infection-control system.

Implant Surgery under Sedation

Dental Implant surgery performed comfortably under sedation.
