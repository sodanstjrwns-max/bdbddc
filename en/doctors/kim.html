Head Director Minsu Kim

A dentist who listens first

Why I became a dentist

As a child, I was afraid of going to the dentist. I wanted to be someone who could ease that fear, even a little. I wanted to care for patients with understanding and empathy so they could feel at ease.

I’ve enjoyed making and fixing things since I was young. Dentistry is the same. I find great fulfillment in precisely restoring damaged teeth and rebuilding those that are missing.

✦

Clinical expertise

I’m a well-rounded clinician with broad experience across fields. I confidently perform Dental Implant surgery, as well as meticulous esthetic Composite Resin treatments and Root Canal Treatment.

"If another clinic said you’re missing teeth and a Denture is your only option, please come see us."
I have extensive experience achieving good outcomes even in complex Dental Implant cases.
✦

Care Philosophy

Provide only the care you truly need, earn your trust with clear explanations, and deliver treatment precisely and promptly.

What matters most to me is listening to you first. The starting point of care is Diagnosis → Empathy → Planning → Explanation—a four-step process.

Only after fully understanding what discomfort you feel and what you’re worried about can an accurate diagnosis and treatment plan be made.

✦

Prevention and Innovation

There is something I always emphasize to my patients. The importance of oral hygiene I strongly recommend using dental floss and interdental brushes. Prevention is the best care.

I continuously study digital systems to increase the accuracy of Dental Implant surgery, shorten surgical time, and minimize your discomfort.

✦

About Me

My MBTI is ISFJ . I’m meticulous and a bit of a perfectionist, so I put great care into each treatment. I value punctuality and respect your time.

My hobbies are singing in a choir, surfing, scuba diving, and computer coding. I find the logical problem-solving in coding similar to how I plan treatments.

There’s just one thing I hope for.
For patients to "They really listen here; I feel at ease"—and truly feel it.
Building that trust is my goal.

Interview with the Director

A message from Head Director Dr. Minsu Kim

Which areas of care are you most confident in?

I’m an “all-rounder” as a clinician. My experience spans from Dental Implant surgery to meticulous esthetic Composite Resin treatments and Root Canal Treatment. In particular, I have many experiences achieving good outcomes in complex Dental Implant cases that others considered difficult.

What do you consider most important when treating patients?

Listening to the patient first. Through the four-step process of ‘Diagnosis → Empathy → Planning → Explanation,’ I create a treatment plan only after fully understanding what discomfort you feel and what concerns you have.

Any advice you’d especially like to share with patients?

I emphasize the importance of oral hygiene. I strongly recommend using floss and interdental brushes. Prevention is the best care. With regular checkups and proper oral care habits, you can keep your teeth healthy for a long time.
