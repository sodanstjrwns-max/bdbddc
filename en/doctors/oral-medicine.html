Oral Medicine

Oral MedicineWhat is it?
A specialty that diagnoses and treats conditions of the orofacial (oral and facial) region without surgery.

Oral Medicine is a dental specialty, TMJ (Jaw Joint) disorders, orofacial pain, teeth grinding/clenching, oral mucosal diseases, dry mouth which we diagnose and treat.
Instead of surgery, medications, physical therapy, behavioral therapy, and oral appliance therapy using these non-surgical approaches.

In Oral Medicine, Services We Provide

Key conditions treated by Oral Medicine specialists

TMJ (Jaw Joint) disorder (TMD)

We diagnose and treat symptoms such as clicking when opening, jaw pain, or limited mouth opening. We use splints, physical therapy, and medications.

Orofacial Pain

We evaluate facial, jaw, and oral pain of unknown cause, neuralgias, and myofascial pain, and provide non-surgical treatment.

Teeth grinding and clenching (bruxism)

We manage tooth wear, jaw pain, and headaches from sleep bruxism and daytime clenching. We prescribe stabilization splints (night guards), among other therapies.

Oral mucosal diseases

We diagnose and treat a range of conditions affecting the oral mucosa, including canker sores (aphthous ulcers), lichen planus, leukoplakia, and oral candidiasis.

Dry mouth

This is mouth dryness caused by reduced saliva. We identify causes such as medication side effects and autoimmune diseases and provide appropriate management.

Snoring and Sleep Apnea

We provide non-surgical treatment for snoring and obstructive sleep apnea using oral appliances (MADs).

What is an Oral Medicine specialist?

A dentist who takes a medical, non-surgical approach to oral diseases

Oral Medicine specialistis a dentist who, after graduating from a 6-year dental program, trains in a university hospital Oral Medicine department for a 3-year residency and must pass the specialty board exam to be certified.

During training, they study TMJ (Jaw Joint) disorders, orofacial pain, oral mucosal pathology, sleep medicine, and dry mouth in depth.

Unlike other dental specialties that mainly focus on surgery, Oral Medicine uses a non-surgical, medical approach to manage conditions of the orofacial region and considers how systemic diseases relate to oral health.
