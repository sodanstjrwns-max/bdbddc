About the Comprehensive Care Center

At Seoul BD Dental, the Comprehensive Care Center includes a board-certified Integrated Dentistry specialist as part of a three-doctor team that provides wisdom tooth extraction, comprehensive care, and oral surgery, covering the full scope of dentistry.

Led by Dr. Jonghun Choi, Director of the Comprehensive Care Center, we provide one-stop care across a wide range of treatments. If you’re not sure which dental treatment you need, start at the Comprehensive Care Center.

Comprehensive Care Center Frequently Asked Questions

Start with an oral exam at the Comprehensive Care Center, and we’ll guide you to the right specialty based on your condition. Center Director Jonghun Choi will create an integrated treatment plan.

Not every wisdom tooth must be extracted. After an accurate diagnosis, we determine whether extraction is needed. We recommend extraction for impacted or horizontally impacted wisdom teeth, or when there are cavities or gum inflammation.

A specialist in integrated dentistry diagnoses and treats the full scope of dental care in an integrated way. It is an official specialty recognized by the Ministry of Health and Welfare, enabling one-stop care for complex oral problems.

Bite on gauze for 2 hours after extraction. Avoid hot foods, alcohol, and strenuous exercise that day. Take your prescribed medications, and visit us if severe pain or swelling occurs.
