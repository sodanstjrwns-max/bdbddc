Wisdom Tooth Extraction?

Wisdom Tooth extraction is a procedure to surgically remove Wisdom Teeth that are impacted in the gums or have erupted abnormally (third molars) through an oral surgical procedure. 3D CTWith it, we pinpoint exact locations and perform extraction safely.

Impacted Wisdom Teeth, horizontally impacted teeth, even those close to the nerve—safe extraction with 3D CT analysis.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction by Type

Tailored extraction based on the condition of your Wisdom Teeth

Normally Erupted Wisdom Tooth

A Wisdom Tooth that has erupted normally but is hard to keep clean.

  • Simple extraction
  • Short procedure time
  • Quick recovery
Recommended for: Normal eruption, Cavity present

Horizontally impacted wisdom tooth

A wisdom tooth that grows sideways. It can cause cavities or inflammation in adjacent teeth.

  • Surgical extraction
  • Bone removal required
  • Careful surgery
Recommended: Horizontal impaction, affects adjacent teeth

Wisdom tooth close to the nerve

A high-difficulty wisdom tooth close to the inferior alveolar nerve.

  • 3D CT required
  • Minimize nerve injury
  • Specialist-performed surgery
Recommended: High-difficulty, nerve-adjacent

Wisdom tooth extraction Process

Knowing each step in advance takes the fear away

CT analysis Anesthesia Extraction Recovery
1

Detailed 3D CT analysis

We precisely analyze the wisdom tooth position, root shape, and its distance from the inferior alveolar nerve using 3D CT. We accurately determine the impaction type and level of difficulty.

About 10 minutes Painless 5th-floor Diagnostic Center
We explain the extraction difficulty in advance based on the CT results. Once you know "how it will be removed," it's not so scary!
2

Anesthesia (sedation optional)

We provide thorough local anesthesia. If you have significant dental anxiety or are removing four at once, you may choose sedation.

About 5 minutes A small pinch (anesthetic injection) Sedation available
If you choose sedation, everything is completed while you sleep. Recommended for those with severe dental anxiety!
3

Safe extraction

We use minimally invasive techniques to minimize gum and bone trauma during extraction. Nerve protection is our top priority.

About 10~30 minutes per tooth Painless (under anesthesia) Minimally invasive technique
With proper anesthesia, there is no pain during the procedure. You may only feel pressure.
4

Bleeding Control & Recovery Care

We carefully suture the extraction site and control bleeding. You’ll receive prescriptions for recovery and detailed aftercare instructions.

About 10 minutes (including instructions) Throbbing once the anesthesia wears off Pain medication prescribed
On the day of extraction, stick to soft foods and apply ice packs! Most people can return to normal activities in about 3–5 days.

Extraction time About 10–30 minutes per tooth; you can go home the same day
