What is an inlay?

An inlay is made by precisely taking an impression of the area damaged by a cavity, then Ceramic) or gold are custom-fabricated and bonded in place. It is more durable than Composite Resin and suitable for larger cavities.

Medium to large cavities are treated with precisely fabricated restorations. Ceramic, gold, Zirconia and other materials provide long-term durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions.

Composite Resin is Direct filling(same-day treatment), whereas an inlay is a precisely fabricated restoration (2 visits). For larger cavities, an inlay is more suitable and offers greater durability.

Ceramic: 10-15 years; gold inlay: 15-20 years or longer.

With adequate anesthesia, there is no pain during treatment. You may experience slight sensitivity while the temporary filling is in place.

An inlay restores only the decayed portion (partial restoration), while a Crown covers the entire tooth (full coverage). If there is enough healthy tooth remaining, an inlay Tooth reductionrequires less and is therefore more conservative.

At the margin between the inlay and the tooth, Secondary cavity (recurrent decay)can occur. Regular checkupand proper brushing habits can prevent this; flossing is also important.
