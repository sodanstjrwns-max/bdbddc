What is an IV Sedation Implant?

An IV Sedation Implant involves administering intravenous sedatives and performing implant surgery while you are in a sleep-like state. Unlike general anesthesia, it is conscious sedation with spontaneous breathing, so no ventilator is needed, and you can return to normal activities in 30~60 minutes after surgery.
