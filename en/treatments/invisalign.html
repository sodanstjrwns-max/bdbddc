InvisalignWhat is it?

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth using clear plastic aligners. Unlike traditional metal brackets, it’s barely noticeable and can be removed for meals and brushing, minimizing inconvenience.

Seoul BD Dental is an Invisalign Diamond Provider. A Seoul National University–trained orthodontist provides personalized care at our dedicated Orthodontic Center on the 1st floor.

Treatment Journey Guide

Invisalign Treatment Process

A structured 6-step process from consultation to retention

Exam Planning Fabrication Fitting Progress Completion
1

Comprehensive Consultation & Exam

3D CT, intraoral scanner, and bite (occlusion) analysis to accurately evaluate your current condition. We’ll advise you on orthodontic suitability and the expected timeline.

About 40 minutes Painless Orthodontic Center (4th floor)
We’ll show you a before-and-after orthodontic simulation video. "My teeth will change like this?" See for yourself!
2

ClinCheck 3D treatment planning

With digital simulation, you can preview the final orthodontic result. We show the tooth-movement sequence in step-by-step 3D.

About 1~2 weeks (planning phase) 3D simulation
We can revise the plan until you’re satisfied with the final result. Decide together with the doctor.
3

Custom appliance fabrication

At Invisalign headquarters (USA), your custom clear aligners appliance is fabricated.

About 2 weeks (fabrication time) Made at U.S. headquarters
No visit is needed during this time. We’ll contact you when the aligners arrive.
4

Aligner fitting & care instructions

We place your first set of clear aligners and carefully teach you how to insert/remove and care for them. Attachments are also placed at this visit.

About 30~40 minutes Mild pressure Wear 20~22 hours per day
You may feel mild soreness for the first 2~3 days—this is a good sign that your teeth have started moving!
5

Regular visits & progress checks

Visit every 4~6 weeks to check your orthodontic progress and switch to the next aligner set. We carefully make sure everything is proceeding as planned.

About 15 minutes per visit Every 4~6 weeks Change aligners every 2 weeks
Remove aligners for meals and brushing, and wear them the rest of the time. Wear time determines your results.
6

Orthodontic treatment complete & Retainers

After completing treatment, we place retainers to keep your results stable. Your new smile is complete!

Retainers for 6 months~1 year Painless New smile complete
Orthodontic treatment complete! Wear your retainers as directed to keep your beautiful smile for the long term.

Total treatment duration About 6–18 months (varies by program)

Invisalign is orthodontic treatment that uses clear plastic aligners. Unlike traditional wire braces, they’re barely visible and removable, so eating and brushing are comfortable. It’s the most widely used clear-aligner system, chosen by over 17 million people worldwide.

Typically 12–18 months, and simple limited cases can be completed within 6 months. With ClinCheck (3D simulation), you can preview the expected duration and results before starting treatment.

You may feel slight pressure for 1–3 days after switching to a new set, but discomfort is far less than with wire braces. Most people adapt without disrupting daily life, and pain relievers are rarely needed.

It works for most cases from mild crowding to moderate malocclusion. However, severe skeletal discrepancies or very complex cases may be better suited to traditional braces. The exact plan is determined after a specialist’s evaluation.

Clean them daily with lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush. Avoid hot water, which can cause warping. Using a dedicated aligner cleaner can keep them even more hygienic.

Because you remove the aligners to eat, there are no food restrictions—one of the biggest advantages over wire braces. Just brush after meals and put them back in.
