What is Composite Resin Treatment?

Composite resin treatment is a procedure where, after removing decay, a tooth-colored composite resinis used to fill and restore the tooth. It can be completed the same day, with minimal Tooth preparationfor a natural-looking result.

We restore cavities to match the exact color of your natural teeth. Small cavities are finished in a single same-day visit.

Do you have these concerns right now?

Many people have the same concerns

"The cavity is small—do I really need to treat it?" Even small cavities will get bigger if left untreated
"Does composite resin last long?" 5~10 years or more, longer with regular checkups
"Will it be noticeable after treatment?" A perfect match to your natural tooth color

Pricing

Cheonan cavity treatment (composite resin) pricing

"How much is composite resin treatment in Cheonan?" — At Seoul BD Dental, composite resin cavity treatment is 50,000 KRW–250,000 KRW (approx. $37–$183) depending on the area and size.

Treatment itemPriceNotes
Back surface cavity of a front tooth (per tooth)50,000 KRW (approx. $37)
Open side-surface cavity (per tooth)70,000 KRW (approx. $51)
Molar chewing surface — small area100,000 KRW (approx. $73)
Cavity at the tooth neck near the gumline (per tooth)100,000 KRW (approx. $73)
Molar chewing surface — large area250,000 KRW (approx. $183)
Cavity between teeth (per surface)250,000 KRW (approx. $183)

Composite resin on permanent teeth for children age 12 and under is covered by Korean NHIS. If decay is deep and reaches the nerve, Root Canal Treatment (covered by Korean NHIS) may be required, followed by a Crown. Before treatment, we’ll provide a written estimate based on the number and size of the cavities.
