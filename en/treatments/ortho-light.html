What is Light Orthodontics?

Light Orthodontics is an affordable program for mild malocclusion. Using a streamlined 14-step appliance set, it addresses front-tooth alignment, mild crowding, and retreatment within 6~12 months. For those who do not require full orthodontics, it delivers satisfying results at a reasonable cost. Includes 1 refinement.

Structured 6-step treatment process

From consultation through retention

1

Detailed consultation & 3D exam

3D CT, an intraoral scanner, and occlusion analysis to accurately assess your current condition.

2

Orthodontic treatment planning

Based on the exam results 클리피씨/클라리티울트라, we select the optimal appliance and finalize your orthodontic plan.

3

Bracket bonding

We precisely bond ceramic brackets to the tooth surfaces. Takes about 1 hour.

4

Regular visits & wire adjustments

We replace the wire every 4–8 weeks to move your teeth.

5

Appliance removal

Once we reach your treatment goals, we remove the brackets.
