Dental ImplantWhat is it?

A Dental Implant replaces the root of a missing tooth Titanium After placing the artificial root in the jawbone, an artificial tooth (Crown) is placed. This dental treatment can restore natural-like function and esthetics, and with proper care it can last for the long term.

6 dedicated operating rooms, 2 recovery rooms — a collaborative system of 14 Seoul National University–trained specialists Bone Graft (GBR) to full-mouth reconstructionPlus, we promise safe Dental Implants 365 days a year.

Dental Implant under sedation

Have your procedure comfortably under sedation. Especially recommended for patients with dental anxiety.

  • Fully resolves fear of surgery
  • Pain-free surgical experience
  • Simultaneous placement of multiple implants
  • Specialized anesthesia system in place
Recommended for: Dental phobia, multiple placements, older adults

Flapless Implant

We perform minimally invasive surgery without gum incisions. You can return to normal activities the same day.

  • Surgery without gum incisions
  • Fast recovery (same-day return to routine)
  • Minimal bleeding and swelling
  • Use of a digital guide
Recommended for: Fast recovery, busy working professionals

Navigation-Guided Implant

Using 3D CT-based simulation, we achieve precise placement with 0.1mm accuracy.

  • 3D CT-based simulation
  • Precise placement within 0.1mm
  • Significantly reduced surgery time

Sinus Lift

When the upper jaw bone is lacking, we reinforce the sinus space with bone to create a foundation for Dental Implant placement.

Immediate Implant (placed at time of extraction)

Immediate-Loading Implant

A temporary tooth is placed the same day as implant placement. Get back to your routine immediately with no gap.

Hybrid Full-Arch Implant (All-on-4/All-on-6)

A Dental Implant is a treatment in which a titanium artificial screw is placed into the jawbone where a tooth was lost (the alveolar bone), and an artificial tooth (Crown) is attached on top. Because it restores function and esthetics closest to natural teeth, it is widely recognized as the best treatment for missing teeth today.

During the procedure, adequate local anesthesia is provided, so there is little to no pain. At Seoul BD Dental, Dental Implants can also be performed under Sedation Dentistry, so even those with dental phobiacan be treated comfortably. Mild swelling and discomfort may occur for 2~3 days after the procedure, but these are well controlled with prescribed medications.

In general, the lower jaw (mandible) takes about 2~3 months, and the upper jaw about 4~6 months. If a bone graft is needed, additional time may be required. At Seoul BD Dental, same-day implants (immediate placement) are also possible, and we plan the optimal treatment based on your condition.

Most adults are candidates for Dental Implants, but conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes, severe osteoporosis, or ongoing cancer treatment require prior evaluation. Even if the jawbone is insufficient, implants may be possible with a Bone Graft (GBR). A precise plan is confirmed after a CT scan and a consultation with our specialist.

For patients aged 65 and older, up to two Dental Implants in a lifetime are covered by Korean NHIS. The patient share is about 30% (around 500,000–600,000 KRW), and for low-income beneficiaries it is 10%. For those under 65, Dental Implants are non-covered (out-of-pocket); exact fees are provided during consultation.

On the day of the procedure, it’s best to eat soft foods (porridge, soup, etc.) on the opposite side. Regular meals are generally possible after about 1~2 weeks, and after the final restoration is placed you can eat nearly all foods. However, it’s best to avoid hard ice or bones—even with natural teeth.

Pricing

Cheonan dental implant pricing — out-of-pocket fee schedule

"How much do dental implants cost in Cheonan?" — At Seoul BD Dental, a dental implant is 800,000–1,600,000 KRW (approx. $580–$1,170) per tooth. Prices vary by implant fixture brand and whether a bone graft is needed.
