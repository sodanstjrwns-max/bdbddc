Before choosing treatment, start with what worries you

Cheonan glownate laminate veneersCan you change your smile without regretting what was removed?

You may hide a chipped edge or an uneven colour, yet feel more worried about healthy tooth removal than about the appearance itself. Photographs can make veneers look like a simple change of surface. Your decision deserves more: the reason for the concern, alternatives that preserve tooth structure and the reality of future maintenance.

Could whitening or bonding be enough?

Possibly, depending on whether the concern is colour, shape, position or damage.

03

Will veneers last permanently?

No individual lifespan or freedom from repair can be promised.

02 / Benefits and burdens

Benefits and burdens

What may improve

Veneers can change selected aspects of tooth shape and visible colour.

What to weigh up

Preparation may be irreversible and later repair or replacement may be needed.

The question that helps you choose

Compare the desired change with the amount of tooth alteration and less invasive alternatives.

03 / Make sense of your own situation

What changes the choice of treatment?

01

Identify whether the concern is colour, shape or position

Whitening, composite bonding and orthodontics solve different problems. If colour is the main concern, covering the surface may not be the first choice. If tooth position is the issue, moving the tooth and changing its visible shape are different approaches. Ask what is causing the appearance you want to change.

02

Discuss preparation without assuming “no-prep”

A veneer covers part of the tooth surface. The amount of preparation depends on the existing tooth, desired shape and clinical conditions. Avoid choosing from a universal thickness or no-removal promise. Ask what tissue would be altered, whether that is reversible and what would happen if the veneer needs replacement later.

03

Look beyond the first photograph

Bite contacts, cleaning at the edges, gum condition and habits such as grinding affect the plan. Veneers can chip, debond or require replacement; whitening will not change their colour in the same way as natural teeth. Discuss trial appearance, shade decisions and the opportunity to communicate concerns before final bonding.

04 / Treatment and everyday life

Plan for the time between appointments, too.

Keep routine checks and clean the veneer edges carefully.

Report a loose or damaged veneer and discuss grinding or clenching before treatment.

Costs, time and follow-up

Ask for a plan you can understand and manage.

What does the estimate include?

Ask which examinations, temporary stages, final treatment, retainers or maintenance, and repairs are included. The scope depends on your findings and chosen plan. A headline price cannot describe every person’s total cost. Ask how a change in the plan would be explained and agreed before it goes ahead.

How will visits fit into my life?

Bring previous records if you have them, your medication list and the practical limits on travel or time off work. Ask what each visit is for, what may change the timetable, and who to contact if something feels wrong. You do not need to promise to start treatment at the first conversation.

05 / Questions worth asking again

Uncertainty is a good reason to ask another question.

Does Glownate mean no tooth preparation?

No blanket promise should be assumed from a treatment name. The proposed preparation must be explained for each tooth.

Can veneers replace orthodontics?

They change the visible surface, while orthodontics changes tooth position. The appropriate choice depends on the underlying problem.

Can I whiten them later?

Whitening affects natural teeth differently from restorations. Coordinate shade plans before making a veneer.

What if I dislike the shape after fitting?

Ask how shape and colour are assessed before final bonding and what changes are feasible at each stage.

Take these questions to your consultation
  • Which part of my concern cannot be solved by whitening or bonding?
  • How much tooth structure would be altered?
  • What repair or replacement responsibilities should I expect?
