Even after Teeth Whitening why the color doesn’t lighten

Staining and discoloration are different issues. That’s because their locations differ.

This is resolved with Teeth Whitening

Extrinsic staining — a surface problem

  • Coffee · black tea · wine · curry · tobacco
  • Pigments on the enamel surfaceare attached.
  • When the bleaching agent breaks down the pigments, the teeth return to their original color.

Soft Whitening (30 min/session) 49,000 KRW (approx. $36) · Hard Whitening (2 sessions, 60 min) 80,000 KRW (approx. $58)
Learn more about Teeth Whitening →

Whitening has its limits.

Intrinsic discoloration — an internal problem

  • Tetracycline · severe fluorosis · mottled teeth
  • A single tooth darkened after Root Canal Treatment
  • As you age, enamel thins and the underlying color shows through the yellow of the dentin

The color inside the toothis on the inside, so bleaching the surface doesn’t change the internal color.

In short — You can’t change the internal color by cleaning the surface. So instead of trying to "erase" it, "cover" methodsare what we consider. And there are different types of covering methods.

There are four ways to improve your front teeth.

Laminate Veneer isn’t always the answer. The right solution depends on the problem.

If it’s only the color at issue,

Teeth Whitening is the right choice.

Don’t get a Laminate Veneer. There’s no reason to grind down teeth for an issue that can be solved for 49,000–80,000 KRW (approx. $36–$58).

AlignmentIf this is the issue,

Orthodontics comes first.

If you grind the teeth to fix what should be solved by moving them, the thickness ends up looking awkward. Invisalign is offered on our 1st floor.

If it’s just one or two slightly

Cosmetic Composite Resin (Bonding)is the better choice

150,000 KRW (approx. $110) is enough. There’s almost no tooth reduction. For small chips or gaps, we recommend starting with this option first.

When color and shape both matter

that’s when Laminate Veneer is right.

Color and shape both are both an issue, and the discoloration that does not respond to whitening— in that case, Laminate Veneer addresses both with the least tooth reduction.

Seoul BD Dental’s principle is “Start with the least invasive option.” Teeth Whitening → Cosmetic Composite Resin → Laminate Veneer → Crown in that order, and if an earlier step solves it, we don’t recommend the later ones. So Where do you fit?

If two or more of the items below apply, it’s time to get a Laminate Veneer consultation

  • Teeth Whitening does not improve intrinsic discoloration(tetracycline staining · severe fluorosis · mottled enamel)
  • The front teeth’s shape, length, or alignment is asymmetric or shortened by wear
  • Gap between the front teeth (Diastema) is bothering you
  • Mild Malocclusion — not to the point of wanting Orthodontics
  • When the color or shape of existing Composite Resin or restorations is awkwardly mismatched with your natural teeth
  • Wedding · interview · photoshoot, etc. the date is set when it’s hard to wait for the duration of Orthodontics

If two or more apply — this usually leads to the following question.

“But once you prepare the tooth, you can’t reverse it”

This is the most common—and most valid—question we hear in the consultation room

First concern

“What if the result looks off?”

This can be solved. DSD (Digital Smile Design)to first simulate the final look, temporary Laminate Veneersare placed so you can live with them in your mouth for a few days before we proceed to final fabrication. It’s a see-and-decide process.

Second concern

“Still, you can’t undo what’s been removed, right?”

That’s correct. Removed enamel does not regenerate. We tell you this plainly. That’s why what matters is “how much is removed.”

Labial enamel thickness of the front teeth 0.5~1.0mm
Glownate reduction amount 0.3~0.5mm

0.3~0.5mm is a range that stays within the enamel thickness. If dentin isn’t exposed, tooth sensitivity and the risk of nerve irritation are greatly reduced. Conversely, if the reduction exceeds 1mm and reaches the dentin, from that point it’s no longer a Laminate Veneer but essentially a Crown.

That’s why we tell you the amount of reduction in numbers beforehand, confirm it with temporary Laminate Veneers, and then proceed to final fabrication. Because this isn’t a procedure where you can “try it and undo it if it doesn’t work.”

Tooth reduction · Safety 5

Glownate Laminate Veneer follows a minimal-prep principle. The amount removed depends on your teeth, and we plan treatment to preserve as much natural tooth as possible. If you’re worried about excessive reduction, Glownate is a good choice.

"No-prep" is largely a marketing phrase. In reality, many cases require about 0.2~0.3mm of reduction, and if teeth are protruded or crowded, adding thickness can look unnatural. At Seoul BD Dental, we don’t advertise "no-prep"; we state honestly that 0.3~0.5mm minimal reduction is typical. If your case can truly be solved without reduction, we recommend Teeth Whitening or esthetic Composite Resin first. For more detailed evidence, see Laminate Veneer Complete Guide's "The Truth About No-Prep Laminate Veneers".

Because Glownate Laminate Veneer is minimal-prep, most cases can be done without anesthesia. For sensitive patients, we use local anesthesia, and there is very little pain during the procedure.

Reported side effects include temporary sensitivity, gum irritation, and leakage at the bonding margins. Glownate follows a 0.3~0.5mm minimal-reduction principle and finishes within the enamel to reduce these risks, and DSD simulation lets you preview the result. If you have Teeth Grinding (Bruxism), a night guard is essential. For the kinds of regrets people actually have, see 5 Things People Regret After Laminate Veneersfor case-by-case examples.

Essential care rules for 10+ years of longevity

Before the procedure (from 1–2 weeks prior to the same day)

  • ✓ Complete any pre-treatment for cavities and periodontitis

  • ✓ Preview with DSD simulation, wax-up, and model try-in
  • ✓ Share any history of Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) or clenching in advance → plan a night guard at the same time
  • ✓ Avoid caffeine and nicotine on the procedure day

First 72 hours after the procedure (golden time)

  • ⚠ No hard foods (apples, bones, nuts, ice, squid, etc.)
  • ⚠ Avoid staining foods (coffee, black tea, cola, wine, curry)
  • ⚠ No smoking or e-cigarettes
  • ⚠ If sensitivity occurs, use lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush
  • ⚠ Avoid irritating the gums (harsh brushing) — use a soft toothbrush + floss
  • ⚠ If you have pain or anything unusual, contact us immediately (☎ 041-415-2892)

Long-term maintenance (1 month–10+ years)

  • ✓ Routine checkup every 6 months + Scaling (Dental Cleaning) (required)
  • ✓ 1 free polishing per year (Seoul BD Dental patient service)
  • ✓ Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) patients: Night guard required
  • ✓ For contact sports (martial arts, boxing, etc.), wear a mouthguard
  • ✓ Rinse promptly right after staining foods/drinks
  • ✓ Do not use teeth as tools (nail-biting, opening bottle caps, cutting thread, etc.)

What is “no-prep Laminate Veneer”?

Often advertised (e.g., Lumineers) as requiring almost no reduction, but in reality 0.2~0.3mm reduction is common and teeth can look bulky. Seoul BD Dental does not recommend no-prep.

Minimal-prep Laminate Veneer

0.3~0.5mm reduction. Maintains natural tooth shade and shape; often the most satisfying option in patient feedback.

Zirconia Laminate Veneer

The highest-strength veneer. Suitable for Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) or a history of front-tooth trauma.
