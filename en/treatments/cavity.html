Conservative (Restorative) Treatment

Cavity Treatment, early detection is key

What is cavity treatment?

Cavity treatment removes tooth structure damaged by bacteria and restores it with Composite Resin, Inlay, Crown and similar materials. If found early, it can often be managed with a simple Composite Resin filling; as it progresses, Root Canal Treatmentmay be required.

Depending on how advanced the cavity is, we provide the most appropriate treatment, such as Composite Resin, Inlay, or Crown, and we prioritize preserving your natural teeth.

Do you have any of these symptoms?

Even minor symptoms, if ignored, can lead to more extensive treatment.

Sensitivity to cold foods

Do your teeth feel sensitive or get a sharp twinge when you eat ice cream or drink cold beverages?

Discomfort with sweets

Do you feel discomfort in a specific tooth when eating chocolate or candy?

Visible dark spot

When you look in the mirror, do you see a dark spot or a hole on a tooth?

Pain when chewing

Do you feel pain in a specific tooth when chewing?

Cavity Prevention Tips

Good habits are the best prevention.

Proper Brushing

Brush 3 times a day, within 3 minutes after meals, for at least 3 minutes, using proper technique.

Floss and Interdental Brushes

A toothbrush alone can’t prevent cavities between teeth.

Regular Checkups

Get regular checkups every 6 months to detect cavities early.

Fluoride Application

Regular fluoride application strengthens enamel and helps prevent cavities.

Cutting Down on Sugar

Reduce sugary foods like candy and soda.
