What is a Flapless Implant?

Flapless Implant(Flapless) is a minimally invasive surgical method that places the implant through only a small 3~4mm opening, without a scalpel incision in the gums. Bleeding, swelling, and pain are minimal, and no sutures are needed.

Do any of these concerns sound familiar?

Many people share the same concerns.

"I don't want my face to swell up after surgery" No incision means minimal swelling
"I need to get back to work quickly" Able to return to daily routine the same day
"I'm afraid of having my gums cut with a scalpel" No incision—just a small opening
"I'm worried about bleeding after surgery" Minimal bleeding, no sutures needed
"A long recovery would be a problem for me" 2~3 times faster recovery than conventional methods
"Do I have to come back just to remove stitches?" No stitches at all—no extra visit needed

A systematic 6-step treatment process

From precise diagnostics to final restorations

1

3D CT & intraoral scan

We precisely analyze the jawbone condition, nerve location, and adjacent teeth.

2

Digital surgical guide design

We plan the optimal placement angle, depth, and position, and fabricate the guide.

3

Guide placement & implant placement

We seat the guide and place the implant precisely by making only a small hole.

4

Placement check

X-rayConfirm the position and angle. No sutures needed.

5

Osseointegration period

The implant and bone integrate over 2~4 months.

6

Final restoration placement
