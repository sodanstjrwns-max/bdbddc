Apicoectomy Steps

Performed precisely with microsurgical techniques

1

3D CT for precise diagnosis

We analyze the extent of infection and surrounding anatomy in 3D.

2

Anesthesia & gum incision

After adequate anesthesia, we incise the gum in the area.

3

Infected tissue & root-end removal

Under a microscope, we remove the infected tissue and the apical 3mm of the root.

4

MTA retrograde filling

We seal the resected root end with an MTA retrograde filling.

5

Suturing & recovery

We suture the gum and remove the stitches about 1 week later.

Root Canal Treatment approaches from the top of the tooth (Crown) but an apicoectomy involves making a gum incision to directly remove the root tip. It is performed for root-end infections that cannot be resolved with Root Canal Treatment.
