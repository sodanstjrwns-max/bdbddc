What is Clippy-C?

Clippy-C, developed by TOMY (Japan), is a self-ligating ceramic bracketIt is. Conventional braces secure the archwire with elastomeric rings or ligature wires, but Clippy-C uses a rhodium-coated clip built into the bracket itselfIt automatically holds the wire. This difference dramatically reduces friction, enabling faster tooth movement with less discomfort.

클리피씨6 key features

Based on official specifications from 토미(TOMY)

Self-ligating system

A rhodium-coated clip built into the bracket automatically secures the archwire. No rubber ties are needed, making it more hygienic with no discoloration.

Minimal friction

In Passive mode, the archwire can slide freely within the slot. Compared with conventional ligation, friction is dramatically reduced.

Faster tooth movement

Lower friction = more efficient force delivery. With the same force, teeth can move faster, helping shorten overall treatment time.

Less pain

Designed to avoid excessive forces, so post–wire-change discomfort is less than with conventional braces. Patient satisfaction is higher.

Excellent aesthetics

Tooth-colored ceramic body + rhodium-coated clip make it less noticeable. Hardly noticeable at normal conversation distance.

Longer appointment intervals

Visits every 6~8 weeks are sufficient. Easy clip open/close also shortens wire change time compared with conventional braces.

Structured 6-step 클리피씨 orthodontic process

Care you can trust from consultation to retention

1

Detailed consultation & 3D diagnostics

We precisely assess your teeth and jawbone with 3D CT, a digital intraoral scanner, and occlusion analysis. The orthodontist will recommend the best treatment planWe will explain it to you.

2

Establishing the orthodontic plan

Based on the digital analysis, we design the 클리피씨 bracket positions, wire sequence, and expected treatment duration.

3

Bonding the 클리피씨 brackets

We precisely bond ceramic brackets to the tooth surfaces. It takes about 1 hour, and the bonding procedure itself is painless.

4

Regular visits & wire adjustments (every 6~8 weeks)

Thanks to self-ligating clips, wire changes are quick and simple. Compared to traditional braces, visit intervals are longer and chair time is shorter.

5

Appliance removal

Once the target outcome is achieved, we safely remove the brackets and finish by cleaning the tooth surfaces.

6

Retainer & regular follow-ups

To maintain your orthodontic results, Retainer is placed. Routine checkupshelp maintain your alignment.
