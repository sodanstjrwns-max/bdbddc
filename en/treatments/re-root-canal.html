Root Canal Retreatment Process

Precise step-by-step treatment

1

Detailed 3D CT analysis

We precisely analyze the cause of infection and the root canal anatomy with 3D CT.

2

Removal of previous filling material

Under a microscope, we safely remove the previous filling material and posts.

3

Re-cleaning & disinfection of canals

We locate missed canals and thoroughly clean and disinfect any remaining infection.

4

Precise re-sealing

We precisely seal the canals with biocompatible materials such as MTA.

5

Crown Crown replacement

We fabricate and place a new crown to protect the tooth.
