What is preventive dentistry?

Preventive dentistry is the field that maintains oral health before disease occurs. Regular checkups, Scaling (Dental Cleaning), Fluoride application, Sealant to help prevent cavities and gum disease.

With preventive programs such as regular checkups, fluoride application, and sealants, we help prevent cavities and gum disease before they start.

Proper oral care

At-home prevention tips

Bass method brushing

Hold the brush at a 45° angle to the gumline and use small vibrating strokes.

Flossing

Clean between your teeth with floss once a day.

Mouthwash

Finish with a mouthwash after brushing.

Cut back on sugar

Reduce your intake of sugary drinks and candy.

Quit smoking

Smoking is a major cause of gum disease.

Regular dental visits

Visit the dentist every 6 months for a checkup.
