What is emergency dental care?

Emergency dental care is immediate care for sudden tooth pain, Tooth fracture, trauma, and other urgent oral problems. At Seoul BD Dental, we treat patients 365 days a year, so emergency care is available on holidays and weekends.

Sudden tooth pain, tooth fracture, Restoration came off etc. Dental emergenciesare handled promptly 365 days a year.

What are the warning signs of pulpitis?

Warning signs that appear before a sudden, severe toothache—knowing them in advance can prevent an emergency.

Easy-to-understand dental explainers

In an emergency Self-care

Do this before you visit

When a tooth is knocked out

  • Place the tooth in milk
  • Do not touch the root
  • If you arrive within 30 minutes, the chance of replantation increases ↑

When bleeding is severe

  • Bite on clean gauze and apply pressure for 30 minutes
  • Keep your head slightly elevated
  • If bleeding doesn't stop, come in immediately
