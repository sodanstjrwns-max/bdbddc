Why is Seoul BD Dental a dedicated Teeth Whitening Center?

6 ways we’re different from the average clinic

Precise VITA shade measurement

Before, during, and after treatment, we record the exact shade using the VITA Classical 16-shade guide. We show the change with objective data.

Precise diagnosis of discoloration causes

Staining, tetracycline, fluorosis, aging, discoloration after Root Canal Treatment, etc. and their causes must be accurately diagnosed to choose the right whitening method.

Triple gum-protection protocol

We protect your soft tissues in 3 steps: gingival barrier (rubber dam or light-cure gel) → lip/cheek barrier → application of the agent, so the whitening gel does not touch the soft tissues.

Designed to minimize sensitivity

We minimize post-whitening sensitivity with pre- and post-fluoride application, personalized control of concentration and time, and a desensitizer.

Doctor’s exam + procedure by a licensed dental hygienist

One of our 14 dentists will accurately diagnose the cause of discoloration and your candidacy, and establish a treatment plan. The whitening procedure itself, which is legally permitted under Korean law, is performed directly by a licensed dental hygienist— this is why we can offer the same quality at a reasonable price.

12-month shade maintenance

Post-procedure shade checks at 3·6·12 months + maintenance-whitening guidance. A home-care kit and regular Scaling (Dental Cleaning) help maintain results for 1~2 years.

Teeth Whitening 5-step Patient Journey

From consultation to maintenance—we designed the key Moments of Truth (MOT) at each step.

1

Comprehensive consultation & shade measurement

Record your current shade with the VITA Classical Shade Guide, analyze discoloration causes (extrinsic/intrinsic), determine candidacy for whitening, and take digital photos (for post-procedure comparison). Takes about 20 minutes.

2

All teeth Scaling (Dental Cleaning) & Polishing

We first remove tartar and extrinsic stains. Polishing smooths the enamel surface for even penetration of the whitening gel. This step alone often improves 2~3 shades.

3

Gum protection & whitening gel application

Isolate soft tissues with a rubber dam or light-cured gingival barrier → place lip/cheek retractors → evenly apply 35% hydrogen peroxide whitening gel. After the dentist’s exam, a licensed dental hygienist performs the procedure.

4

Light activation & gel replacement

Activate the gel with an LED whitening light (wavelength 400~500nm). Replace it every 15 min × 2~3 cycles while monitoring shade changes in real time. If sensitivity occurs, we immediately adjust concentration and time.

5

Shade check & maintenance guidance

Re-measure shade immediately after and at 24 hours; provide before/after comparison photos. Diet guide + home-care kit + schedule for regular check-ups (3·6·12 months).

Pre- & post-treatment Care guide

Key tips to extend the average results by about 1.5 times

Before treatment (1 week~same day)

  • ✓ 1~2 weeks prior: routine check-up ·Scaling (Dental Cleaning) complete
  • ✓ Treat cavities and periodontitis in advance
  • ✓ If you have a history of sensitivity, please let us know in advance
  • ✓ On the day of treatment, avoid caffeine and nicotine
  • ✓ Photograph the locations of restorations (Laminate Veneer·Crown)

48 hours after treatment (golden time)

  • ⚠ Absolutely no staining foods
    (coffee·black tea·cola·wine·curry·soy sauce·blueberries)
  • ⚠ No smoking or vaping
  • ⚠ If sensitivity occurs, use lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush
  • ⚠ We recommend using a desensitizing toothpaste (e.g., GC 톳스)
  • ⚠ Use a lip protectant or Vaseline (to prevent chapped lips)

Long-term maintenance (1~12 months)

  • ✓ Brush after every meal (or rinse)
  • ✓ Rinse promptly after any staining foods
  • ✓ Regular checkups and Scaling (Dental Cleaning) every 6 months
  • ✓ Boost with your home whitening tray 1~2 times per month
  • ✓ Shade check at 3·6·12 months (complimentary)
  • ✓ If the shade of any crowns or other restorations changes, please schedule a separate consult
