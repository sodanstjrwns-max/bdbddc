What is 오스템 CA?

오스템 CA (Calcium-coated Acid-etched) is an implant fixture in which the existing SA (Sandblasted & Acid-etched) surface is additionally coated with a calcium (Ca) ion solution to create an additional coating. Calcium ions bone-forming cells (osteoblasts)promote their activity, leading to faster and more stable early osseointegration than SA.

CA surface treatment Key technology

SA’s physical roughness + calcium’s chemical bonding strength

Calcium ion coating

A calcium (Ca) ion solution is additionally applied to the SA surface. Calcium is a key component of hydroxyapatite (HA), the main constituent of bone, and is highly biocompatible.

Osteoblast activation

Calcium ions promote osteoblast attachment and proliferation on the implant surface. This accelerates new bone formation and improves early stability.

SLA-based rough surface

A micro-rough surface created by dual sandblasting and acid-etching provides an environment where bone cells adhere well.

Proven safety

오스템, Korea’s first implant developer, has 30+ years of clinical data and export records in 70+ countries worldwide.

Faster osseointegration

Compared with the 6~8 weeks typical for SA, the osseointegration period is shortened to 4~6 weeks. Earlier prosthesis placement becomes possible, reducing overall treatment time.

Affordable pricing

Delivers premium-level performance at a reasonable price. A high-value choice with excellent results for the cost.

Questions about 오스템 CA

CA is a product with calcium ions coated onto the SA surface, while BA is a premium option that combines the benefits of SA and HA (hydroxyapatite) coatings. BA is a step above CA and can be applied more broadly across various bone qualities.

With proper oral care and regular checkups, it can last more than 20 years. Osstem provides a 10-year warranty, and in real-world clinical use, lifespans often far exceed this.

If bone is weak, we recommend higher-tier options such as SOI or BA. CA is suitable for patients with good or slightly deficient bone quality. We’ll give a precise recommendation after a CT scan and consultation.

The overall success rate of Osstem Dental Implants has been reported at over 97%, and thanks to the calcium coating that enhances initial stability, CA can be expected to perform even better.
