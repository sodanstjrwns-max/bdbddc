Oral MedicineWhat is it?

Oral Medicine is a dental specialty that diagnoses and treats oral conditions without surgery. TMJ (Jaw Joint) Disorder, chronic orofacial pain, Teeth Grinding (Bruxism), snoring, Dry Mouth (Xerostomia), Oral mucosal diseases are among our specialties.

Certified by the Korean Ministry of Health and Welfare Oral Medicine Specialist provides precise diagnosis and personalized treatment at the level of Seoul National University.
In the Cheonan-Asan area, care by an Oral Medicine Specialist Seoul BD Dental is the only clinic.

Oral Medicine Specialized Care Areas

Have these symptoms? Ask an Oral Medicine Specialist

TMJ (Jaw Joint) Disorder (TMD)

Jaw pain, sounds when opening, limited opening, a feeling that the jaw is dislocating

Chronic orofacial pain

Unexplained chronic pain of the face, jaw, or mouth area, trigeminal neuralgia

Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) · Clenching

Sleep bruxism, daytime clenching, tooth wear · fractures

Snoring · Sleep Apnea

Severe snoring, sleep apnea, oral appliance (MAD) therapy

Dry Mouth (Xerostomia)

Dry mouth sensation, reduced salivary flow, medication side effects

Oral Mucosal Diseases

Recurrent aphthous stomatitis, lichen planus, leukoplakia, oral ulcers

Oral Medicine Care Process

An accurate diagnosis is the start of proper treatment

1

Medical History & Initial Consultation

We review when and how your symptoms began and any prior treatments. We also check your overall health and current medications.

2

Comprehensive Testing & Diagnosis

3D CT, occlusion analysis, TMJ (Jaw Joint) palpation, electromyography (EMG), and other Oral Medicine-specific tests to identify the exact cause.

3

Personalized Treatment Plan

Based on your diagnosis, we create an individualized plan optimized for you. We explain the options, timeline, and costs in detail.

4

Specialized Treatment

We provide treatments tailored to your symptoms, such as splints (occlusal stabilization appliances), medication therapy, physical therapy, Botox, and custom 3D-printed oral appliances.

5

Follow-up & Maintenance

We monitor symptom changes regularly and adjust the plan as needed. We also guide lifestyle and habit changes to help prevent recurrence.

Cheonan Oral Medicine Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions about Oral Medicine care

Yes, at Seoul BD Dental Dr. Byungmin Leeis Cheonan’s only Oral Medicine specialist certified by the Korean Ministry of Health and Welfare. He graduated from Seoul National University School of Dentistry and holds a PhD from its Graduate School of Dentistry, and he specializes in TMJ (Jaw Joint) disorders, chronic orofacial pain, Teeth Grinding (Bruxism), snoring, and dry mouth.

Oral Medicine focuses on TMJ (Jaw Joint) disorders (TMD), chronic orofacial pain, Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) and clenching, snoring and sleep apnea, dry mouth, oral mucosal diseases (canker sores/aphthous ulcers, lichen planus, etc.), trigeminal neuralgia, taste disorders, and bad breath (halitosis)—providing expert diagnosis and non-surgical treatment for conditions in the mouth.

Oral Medicine is a non-surgical field that treats TMJ, pain, and mucosal diseases with medications, physical therapy, and oral appliances, while Oral Surgery specializes in surgical care such as Wisdom Tooth extractions, Dental Implant surgery, and cyst removal. Seoul BD Dental has specialists in both fields on site.

Yes. For TMJ pain, noises, or limited mouth opening, an Oral Medicine specialist provides the most accurate diagnosis. Dr. Byungmin Lee—uses 3D CT, occlusal analysis, and electromyography (EMG) for a precise diagnosis, then provides customized treatment such as splints, medications, and physical therapy.

Yes. Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) and clenching are core areas of Oral Medicine. At Seoul BD Dental, we fabricate custom 3D-printed / night guards(occlusal stabilization splints), and we also provide Botox therapy, habit modification, and stress management for comprehensive care.

Yes. Our Oral Medicine specialist treats them by fabricating a mandibular advancement device (MAD). It advances the lower jaw to open the airway and is an effective alternative for patients who have difficulty using CPAP.

Dry mouth is an Oral Medicine specialty. We perform differential diagnosis for causes such as medication side effects, diabetes, Sjögren’s syndrome, and radiation therapy after-effects, and provide personalized care with saliva substitutes, medications, and lifestyle modification.

Recurrent mouth ulcers, sores that do not heal for more than 2 weeks, and white/red patches must be evaluated by an Oral Medicine specialist. Distinguishing them from premalignant lesions such as oral lichen planus and leukoplakia is crucial, and early detection enables effective management.
