What is conscious sedation (IV sedation)?

A sedative is administered through an IV so you can receive dental treatment in a semi-sleep state. Unlike general anesthesia, your breathing and protective reflexes are maintained for safety, yet you remember little to none of the procedure. (dental phobia) this is an ideal option.

Are you afraid of the dentist? With conscious IV sedation, you can comfortably complete all your treatment while you sleep.

When Sedation Dentistry is needed

Dental phobia

People who feel intense fear just from the sounds or smells of dental instruments

  • No memory of the procedure
  • Comfortable care without fear
  • Relieves psychological stress
Recommended for: Dental trauma, severe dental phobia

Multiple-tooth treatment

Dental Implant, extraction, Gum surgery for those who want to have multiple treatments at once

  • Completed in a single visit
  • Save time and money
  • Comfortable even for long procedures
Recommended for: Multiple Dental Implants, complex treatment

Severe gag reflex

Those who have a severe gag reflex when instruments go in the mouth, making treatment difficult

  • Suppresses the gag reflex
  • Comfortable Dental impressions
  • Scaling (Dental Cleaning) also possible
Recommended for: Gagging, difficulty with impressions

Pediatric Sedation Dentistry

When a child’s dental fear and movement make treatment difficult

  • Trauma-free treatment
  • Safe, pediatric-specific protocols
  • Multiple teeth treated in one visit
Recommended for: Pediatric cavities, children with dental anxiety

Sedation Dentistry Process

Safe, step-by-step sedation care

1

Pre-exam & consultation

We review your health status, current medications, and allergies. Then we determine whether Sedation Dentistry is appropriate.

2

Connect monitoring devices

We connect devices to monitor ECG, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and pulse in real time.

3

Sedative administration

We slowly administer a sedative through an IV. Within a few minutes, you enter a comfortable moderate (conscious) sedation state.

4

Dental treatment is performed

We carry out the planned treatment while you remain comfortable. Our dedicated anesthesia team continuously monitors your vital signs.

5

Recovery & Discharge

After treatment, you will rest in the recovery room. Once the sedation has fully worn off, you will go home with your accompanying guardian.

Before Sedation Dentistry Precautions

Please follow these for safe sedation.

1
Begin fasting 8 hours before the procedure

No water, food, gum, etc. (to prevent reflux of stomach contents)

2
Guardian required

You will not be able to drive after the procedure, so please come with a guardian.

3
Tell us about your medications in advance

Please be sure to inform us of any medications you are currently taking, such as blood pressure medication, diabetes medication, or blood thinners (anticoagulants).

4
Wear comfortable clothing to your visit

For the IV line, it's best to wear clothing that allows easy access to your arms.
