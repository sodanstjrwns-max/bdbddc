Sedation Dentistry

While you sleep,
your treatment will be finished

What is conscious IV sedation?

It is a way to receive dental care in a semi-sleep state by administering a sedative through an IV. Unlike general anesthesia, your breathing and reflexes are maintained for safety, and you remember little to none of the procedure, so it is ideal for or dental phobia patients.

Are you afraid of the dentist? With conscious IV sedation, you can comfortably receive all your treatment while you sleep.

When Sedation Dentistry is needed

Dental phobia

People who feel intense fear just from the sounds or smells of dental instruments

  • No memory of the procedure
  • Comfortable care without fear
  • Eases anxiety
Recommended: Dental trauma, severe dental anxiety

Multiple teeth to treat

Dental Implant, extraction, Gum Surgery and other treatments done in one visit

  • Get it done in a single visit
  • Save time and money
  • Stay comfortable even during long procedures
Recommended: Multiple Dental Implants, complex treatment

Severe gag reflex

Those who have severe gagging when instruments go into the mouth, making treatment difficult

  • Gag reflex suppression
  • comfortable Impression Taking
  • Scaling (Dental Cleaning)also possible
Recommended: Gagging, difficulty with impressions

Pediatric Sedation Dentistry

When a child’s dental fear and movement make treatment difficult

  • Trauma-free treatment
  • Safe, child-specific protocols
  • Treat multiple teeth at once
Recommended: Children with cavities; children with dental fear

Sedation Dentistry Process

Safe, step-by-step sedation care

1

Pre-exam & consultation

We check your health status, current medications, and any allergies. We determine whether sedation is appropriate for you.

2

Connect monitoring devices

We connect devices to monitor ECG, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and pulse in real time.

3

Administer sedative

We slowly administer a sedative through an IV. Within a few minutes, you enter a comfortable moderate-sedation state.

4

Begin dental treatment

We perform the planned treatment while you remain comfortable. Our professional anesthesia team continuously monitors your vital signs.

5

Recovery & going home

After treatment, rest in the recovery room. Once the sedative has sufficiently worn off, go home with a guardian.

Before Sedation Dentistry Precautions

Please follow these for safe Sedation Dentistry

1
Fast starting 8 hours before the procedure

No water, food, or gum. (Prevents reflux of stomach contents)

2
Escort required

You will not be able to drive yourself after the procedure, so please come with a guardian.

3
Please disclose your current medications in advance

Be sure to tell us any medications you’re currently taking, such as blood-pressure medicines, diabetes medications, or anticoagulants.

4
Dress comfortably for your visit

It helps to wear clothing with sleeves that allow easy access to your arm for an IV.

Sedation dentistry administers IV sedatives to place you in a semi-sleep state while dental treatment is performed. Unlike general anesthesia, you remain conscious yet do not feel anxiety or pain, and when treatment ends it typically feels as if you’ve just woken up.

Because spontaneous breathing is maintained (unlike general anesthesia), conscious sedation is very safe. At Seoul BD Dental, we continuously monitor blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and heart rate, and experienced specialists perform the procedure.

It’s suitable for people with severe dental anxiety, a strong gag reflex, those who need long appointments (for example, multiple Dental Implants), patients with disabilities who have difficulty cooperating, and children.
