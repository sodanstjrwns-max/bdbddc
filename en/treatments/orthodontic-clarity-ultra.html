What is Clarity Ultra?

Clarity™ Ultra by 3M (now Solventum) is a premium ceramic orthodontic bracketthat uses a ceramic door to hide the archwire, APC™ Flash-Free adhesive technologyto enable clean bonding without adhesive excess. A 0.022-inch MBT™ prescription slot and a stress-concentrating base design allow safe debonding — a top-tier orthodontic appliance that embodies 3M engineering.

Clarity Ultra— 6 key features

Based on 3M official specifications

Ultra-high-translucency ceramic

3M’s premium ceramic has very high translucency that blends naturally with tooth color. It is highly resistant to discoloration, keeping its clarity throughout treatment.

APC Flash‑Free bonding

Adhesive is pre-applied to the bracket base, so there’s no excess upon placement. Bonding time reduced by 40%, bonding failures reduced by 60%.

Strong bond strength

Uniform adhesive application with APC technology minimizes bracket debonding. Helps reduce unnecessary reattachment visits during treatment.

Smooth curved design

Rounded edges and a smooth surface minimize irritation to the lips and cheek lining. Designed for optimal patient comfort.

Safe debonding

The Stress Concentrator Base creates a predictable break point, enabling clean removal at debonding without damaging the tooth.

Precise rotation control

A wide ceramic door encloses the wire for precise rotational control. Passive/active on‑demand ligation provides the right force as needed.

A systematic 6-step Clarity Ultra orthodontic process

Care you can trust, from consultation to retention

1

Comprehensive consult & 3D diagnostics

Using 3D CT, a digital intraoral scanner, and occlusal analysis, we precisely assess your teeth and jawbone. Our orthodontist will recommend the optimal plan and explain it to you.

2

Orthodontic treatment planning

Based on the digital analysis, we design Clarity Ultra bracket positions, the MBT prescription, and the expected treatment duration.

3

APC Flash-Free bonding

We precisely place pre-coated Clarity Ultra brackets on your teeth. Bonding is up to 40% faster than conventional methods, with clean margins and no excess adhesive.

4

Regular visits & wire adjustments (every 4~6 weeks)

We regularly change the wire to move your teeth. The ceramic door enables precise rotational control.

5

Appliance removal (safe debonding)

Thanks to a stress-concentrating base design, brackets separate cleanly at a predictable break point, minimizing enamel surface damage.

6

Retainer & regular maintenance

To maintain your orthodontic results, retainer is placed. Regular checkups help maintain your alignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions about Clarity Ultra orthodontic treatment

Clarity Ultra is a top-tier ceramic orthodontic bracket manufactured by 3M (now Solventum). APC™ Flash-Free adhesive technology keeps bonding clean with no excess adhesive, and the highly transparent ceramic material has minimal discoloration.

APC (Adhesive Pre-Coated) Flash-Free is 3M’s proprietary technology, with adhesive pre-applied on the bracket base. During placement, adhesive does not overflow beyond the bracket (Flash-Free), allowing clean bonding, and bonding time is reduced by about 40% by decreasing steps from 11 to 5. Bond failure rate also decreases by 60% (JCO 2014).

The ceramic material itself is highly resistant to discoloration. Even if you eat curry, coffee, etc., the brackets do not discolor. If elastic ties are used, those may discolor, but we replace them at regular visits.
