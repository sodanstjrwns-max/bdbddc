What is Clarity Ultra?

Clarity™ Ultra by 3M (now Solventum) is a premium ceramic orthodontic bracketIt uses a ceramic door to hide the wire, APC™ Flash-Free bonding technologyfor clean bonding without adhesive excess. With a 0.022-inch MBT™ prescription slot and a stress-concentrating base design for safe debonding — a top-tier orthodontic appliance embodying 3M technology.

Based on 3M official specifications

Ultra-clear ceramic

3M’s premium ceramic has very high translucency, blending naturally with tooth color. It is extremely resistant to discoloration, maintaining clarity throughout treatment.

APC Flash-Free bonding

Adhesive is pre-applied to the bracket base, so there’s no excess upon placement. Bonding time reduced by 40%, bond failure rate reduced by 60%.

Strong bond strength

Uniform adhesive application with APC technology minimizes bracket debonding. It reduces unnecessary reattachment visits during treatment.

Smooth, curved design

Rounded edges and a smooth surface minimize irritation to the lips and cheek mucosa. Designed for optimal patient comfort.

Safe debonding

The Stress Concentrator Base creates a predictable break point, allowing clean removal during debonding without damaging the tooth.

Precise rotational control

A wide ceramic door encloses the wire for precise rotational adjustment. Passive/active on-demand ligation provides force levels tailored to the situation.

Find answers to your questions about Clarity Ultra orthodontics

Clarity Ultra is a top-tier ceramic orthodontic bracket made by 3M (now Solventum). With APC™ Flash-Free adhesive technology, bonding is clean without adhesive overflow, and the high-translucency ceramic hardly discolors.

APC (Adhesive Pre-Coated) Flash-Free is 3M’s proprietary technology with adhesive pre-applied to the bracket base. During placement, the adhesive does not overflow beyond the bracket (flash-free), allowing a clean bond; bonding time is shortened by about 40% by reducing the steps from 11 to 5. Bond failure rate also decreases by 60% (JCO 2014).

The ceramic material itself is highly resistant to discoloration. Even with curry, coffee, and similar foods, the brackets do not discolor. If elastic ligatures are used, they can discolor, but we replace them at regular visits.
