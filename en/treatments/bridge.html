What is a Dental Bridge?

A Dental Bridge is a fixed prosthesis that uses the healthy teeth on both sides of a missing tooth as abutments to connect a replacement tooth. Dental Implantcan be an alternative when a Dental Implant is not feasible, Zirconia/ceramic materials create a natural appearance.

Restores function and aesthetics by connecting the missing-tooth space to the adjacent teeth on both sides.

Do you have these concerns right now?

Many people share the same concerns.

"Is it okay to leave a missing tooth untreated?" Adjacent teeth can shift and bite problems can develop.
"I'm afraid of getting a Dental Implant" A non-surgical option with a Dental Bridge
"How long does it take?" About 2 weeks, 2 visits

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions.

In the long term, a Dental Implant is advantageous; however, if surgery is a concern or you have certain systemic conditions, a Dental Bridge may be more appropriate.

They can last 7~15 years; it depends on maintenance.

Because some structure is removed from the teeth on both sides, they can be weaker than before. However, if the teeth are healthy, many people do well with a bridge for 10 years or more. We can also compare options with a Dental Implant based on your condition.

An interdental brush or Waterpik(oral irrigator) to clean under the bridge every day is important. We will provide detailed instructions after the procedure.

Price Guide

Dental Bridge Pricing

"How much does a bridge cost?" — A bridge is a prosthesis that connects Crowns on the teeth next to a missing tooth. Based on a Zirconia crown, the fee is Zirconia crown 550,000 KRW (approx. $400) per tooth (typically a 3-unit bridge = 1,650,000 KRW (approx. $1,205)).

Treatment ItemPriceNotes
Zirconia Crown (per tooth)550,000 KRW (approx. $400)Dental Bridge (per unit)
3-unit Dental Bridge (for one missing tooth)Approx. 1,650,000 KRW (approx. $1,205)550,000 KRW (approx. $400) per tooth × 3

A Dental Bridge has the drawback of trimming the healthy teeth on both sides; if your jawbone condition is good, we recommend a comparison consult with a Dental Implant (starting at 800,000 KRW (approx. $580)). Seoul National University–trained specialists will guide you honestly to the best option for your teeth.
