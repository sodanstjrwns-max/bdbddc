What is TSIII SOI?

SOI (Super-hydrophilic Osstem Implant) is Osstem’s TSIII fixture with a super-hydrophilic surface treatmentapplied, the top-tier product. It maintains high surface free energy to prevent carbon contamination and, upon contact with blood, forms a rich blood clotto promote rapid osseointegration. It maintains a neutral pH environment, suppressing osteoclasts and maximizing osteoblast activity.

SOI surface treatment Key technologies

Super-hydrophilicity + high surface free energy + neutral pH

Super-hydrophilic surface

Upon contact with blood, the SOI surface spreads rapidly and induces the formation of a rich blood clot. This clot accelerates the first stage of osseointegration.

High surface free energy

A special process keeps the surface free energy high. It prevents adsorption of carbon contaminants, preserving super-hydrophilicity over time.

Neutral pH environment

The Dental Implant surface maintains a neutral pH, suppressing bone-resorbing osteoclast activity and maximizing bone-forming osteoblast activity.

Protein-binding capacity 130-fold

With about 130-fold higher protein-binding than conventional surfaces, plasma proteins rapidly adsorb to the surface, strongly transmitting osteogenic signals.

SLA-based microstructure

The SOI treatment is applied over a micro-rough surface formed by sandblasting and acid etching, securing both mechanical and chemical bonding strength.

Global validation

Since its 2016 launch, it has been sold in over 70 countries with more than 440,000 units. A proven, top-tier fixture chosen by countless clinicians.

Cases where SOI really shines

Older patients

As you age, osseointegration can slow down. SOI’s faster osseointegration supports stable outcomes.

Patients with diabetes or osteoporosis

Those with systemic conditions and reduced bone metabolism. SOI’s ability to activate osteoblasts helps create an optimal environment.

Weaker bone quality (upper molars)

The upper molar area often has softer bone. SOI provides excellent primary stability even in weaker bone.

Patients who want to finish treatment quickly

With about 4 weeks for osseointegration, the time to the final restoration can be significantly shortened.

Complex cases

In complex surgeries with bone grafting, faster osseointegration can improve overall treatment success.
