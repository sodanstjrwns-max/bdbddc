What is a denture?

A denture is a removable artificial replacement that restores function when multiple teeth are missing. Partial Dentureand Full Dentureare available, Dental Implantused as anchors Implant Overdentureis also available.

We provide well-fitting, natural-looking dentures through precise custom fabrication and careful adjustments.

Any of these concerns on your mind?

Many people share the same concerns

"My denture doesn’t fit well and it’s uncomfortable" Precise customization + careful adjustments
"I’m worried my denture will fall out" Secure retention + Implant Overdenture
"I can’t chew food properly" Optimal design based on bite analysis

Denture Pricing — Korean NHIS coverage for ages 65 and over

"How much do dentures cost?" — At Seoul BD Dental, dentures are 1,500,000 KRW per arch (approx. $1,090), and for those aged 65 or over, Korean NHIS coverage applies with a 30% out-of-pocket share.
