What is Gum (Periodontal) Treatment?

Gum (Periodontal) Treatment is Gingivitis, Periodontitis dental procedures that treat these gum diseases. Bleeding gums, swelling, and bad breath mean early treatment is important, Scaling (Dental Cleaning) starting with Periodontal Surgerycarried out step by step.

Bleeding gums, swelling, and tooth sensitivity are early signs of gum disease. Early treatment helps prevent tooth loss.

Gum disease refers to inflammation from dental plaque (plaque) and tartar in the gums and (alveolar bone). It often begins with gum bleeding (gingivitis); if left untreated, the supporting bone is lost, teeth loosen, and they may fall out (periodontitis). It is very common—over 80% of Korean adults experience it.

Bleeding when brushing, swollen and reddened gums, bad breath, teeth looking longer (gum recession), loose teeth, and discomfort when chewing are typical symptoms. Early on, signs can be subtle and easy to miss.

Scaling is covered by Korean NHIS once per year. In general, it’s recommended every 6 months; if your gums aren’t healthy, every 3~4 months is better.

① Scaling (Dental Cleaning) ② Root planing (deep cleaning under the gums) ③ Periodontal surgery(in severe cases, opening the gums to clean) ④ Maintenance (regular checkups). Treatment proceeds step by step, and in most cases non-surgical care is sufficient.

Early gingivitis can be completely cured with Scaling (Dental Cleaning) and proper care. In moderate or advanced periodontitis, lost bone does not fully recover, but with appropriate treatment and maintenance the disease can be stopped and stability maintained. Lifelong maintenance is required.

Consider gum surgery for deep periodontal pockets (6 mm or more) or severe bone loss that do not improve with non-surgical care (Scaling (Dental Cleaning), root planing). The gums are opened to remove deep calculus and inflamed tissue, and a Bone Graft (GBR) may be performed if needed.

① Proper brushing (Modified Bass technique) ② Use floss/interdental brushes daily ③ Scaling (Dental Cleaning) every 6 months ④ Quit smoking (smoking is the biggest risk factor for gum disease) ⑤ Manage diabetes and stress. Quitting smoking alone can greatly reduce your risk of gum disease.
