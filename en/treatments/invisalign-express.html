What is Invisalign Express?

Invisalign Express is a mini package designed to correct very simple alignment issues in the shortest time. Comprising 7 stages of aligners, it corrects minor front-tooth crowding, small gaps, or slight relapse after previous orthodontics in 3~6 months. It’s the most economical option in the Invisalign lineup.
