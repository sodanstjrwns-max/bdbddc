What is the Moderate Orthodontic Program?

The Moderate Orthodontic Program is optimized for moderate malocclusion. With a structured 23-step appliance set, it enables efficient orthodontic correction. It suits cases that don’t require comprehensive full treatment but are hard to solve with simple minor (partial) correction. With precise digital diagnostics, we create the optimal plan, and two refinements are included for detailed finishing.

Systematic 6-step treatment process

From consultation to retention

1

Detailed consultation & 3D diagnostics

Using 3D CT, an intraoral scanner, and occlusion analysis, we accurately assess your current condition.

2

Orthodontic treatment planning

Based on the examination results Clippy-C/Clarity Ultra, from which we select the most suitable appliance and plan your orthodontic treatment.

3

Appliance placement

We precisely bond ceramic brackets to the tooth surfaces. About 1 hour.

4

Regular visits & wire adjustments

We replace the archwire every 4~8 weeks to move your teeth.

5

Appliance removal

We remove the brackets once the desired result is achieved.

6

Retainer & maintenance

Post-orthodontic retainer (retainer) is placed. Regular checkupsMaintain your results.
