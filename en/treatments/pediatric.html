Pediatric DentistryWhat is it?

Pediatric dentistry is the dental specialty that cares for oral health from infancy through adolescence. Primary tooth care, Fluoride Application, Sealant, Pediatric Orthodontics and other treatments tailored to each child's stage of growth.

Three board-certified pediatric dentists provide care at your child's level. Laughing Gas, Sedation Dentistryfor a fear-free dental experience.

Pediatric Dentistry Services

Personalized care for each stage of your child's growth

Children's cavity treatment

We treat cavities in baby teeth and permanent teeth with expertise. We apply behavior guidance techniques tailored to your child's level of cooperation.

  • Behavior guidance techniques
  • Composite Resin·Crown treatment
  • Fluoride application for prevention
  • Baby tooth extraction
Recommended for: Toddlers and children with cavities

Laughing gas (nitrous oxide) sedation

Inhalation of nitrous oxide (N2O) helps reduce anxiety. You remain conscious and receive treatment comfortably.

  • Conscious sedation
  • Quick recovery (5 minutes)
  • Safe procedure
  • Suitable for mild dental anxiety
Recommended for: Mild dental fear, age 3+
Recommended

Pediatric sedation dentistry

While asleep, multiple teeth can be treated in one visit. Suitable for severe anxiety or numerous cavities.

  • Fully asleep
  • Multiple cavities in one visit
  • Dedicated anesthesia team on staff
  • Safety monitoring
Recommended for: Severe dental anxiety, multiple cavities

Pediatric orthodontics

Guide permanent tooth eruption and expand the arch with growth-phase orthodontics. Invisalign The latest orthodontic systems, including First.

  • Invisalign First
  • Arch expansion
  • Permanent tooth eruption guidance
  • Leveraging the growth phase
Recommended for: Growth-phase orthodontics for ages 6~12

Care Journey Guide

Pediatric Dentistry Care Process

Five steps to open your child’s heart and protect healthy teeth

Getting Comfortable Checkup Treatment Prevention Ongoing Care
1

First Visit & Getting Comfortable

We take time to befriend your child. Using the Tell-Show-Do technique, we show the instruments, let them hear the sounds, and help them adjust step by step.

About 10~15 minutes Conducted like playtime Child-only area
Instead of "We’re going to the dentist," say, "We’re going to meet the tooth-brushing teacher"—kids feel more at ease!
2

Comprehensive Exam

panoramic, plus an oral exam to assess the current condition, the treatment planis established. We explain the results to parents in detail.

About 15~20 minutes Painless Includes parent consultation
Even if there are no cavities, we also check the timing of permanent tooth eruption and jaw growth.
3

Treatment

We provide safe care using the best method for your child's condition and anxiety level, such as behavior guidance, nitrous oxide (laughing gas), or Sedation Dentistry.

Varies by treatment Tailored sedation applied 3 pediatric dentists
It's okay if your child cries during treatment. Our pediatric specialists go at your child's pace.
4

Preventive care

Fluoride application, sealants, and other preventive care, plus proper toothbrushing instruction. We also guide parents on home care.

About 15 minutes Painless Fluoride + sealants
For best results, avoid eating for 30 minutes after fluoride application!
5

Regular checkups & growth management

With routine checkups every 3~6 months, we support healthy permanent-tooth development. We also check whether orthodontic treatment is needed.

Every 3~6 months Painless Tailored care for the growth stage
Regular checkups are the start of healthy permanent teeth! They also help your child feel comfortable at the dentist.

Our pediatric dentist expertly manages children’s fear and anxiety. The first visit starts with getting comfortable, without any treatment; if needed, we proceed comfortably with laughing gas or Sedation Dentistry. Even very timid children—don’t worry.

The cost of Sedation Dentistry varies by the procedure and time. We will provide an exact estimate during your consultation, the Fees pagefor details.

When a baby tooth is severely damaged stainless steel crown(SSC) is the most effective option. It protects the tooth until the baby tooth falls out and also maintains the space for the permanent tooth. If you are concerned about appearance, Zirconia prefabricated crowns can also be discussed.

We recommend the first visit between 6 months and 1 year of age, when the first baby tooth erupts. At the latest, schedule a checkup around the first birthday. Getting comfortable with the dentist early can help prevent dental anxiety.

Yes, they must be treated. Leaving cavities in baby teeth untreated can lead to pain and infection and can negatively affect the development of the underlying permanent teeth. Baby teeth also play an important role in maintaining space for the permanent teeth.

A sealant (pit-and-fissure sealant) fills the deep grooves of molars to prevent cavities. It is simple and painless, and for those age 18 and under, permanent molars (1st and 2ndmolars) is covered by Korean NHIS.

Fluoride strengthens the tooth surface and can reduce cavities by 40–60%. Receiving fluoride application every 6 months is effective and is especially recommended for children at high risk for cavities.

At Seoul BD Dental, we use a stepwise desensitization approach called “behavior management.” We don’t proceed with treatment at the first visit; instead, we help your child get comfortable with the dental setting. For severe dental anxiety, Sedation Dentistry is also available.

It’s typical for the permanent tooth to erupt within 6 months to 1 year after a baby tooth falls out. If it hasn’t come in after more than 1 year, X-ray exam should be done to check the position and condition of the permanent tooth. (impacted tooth)or it may be congenitally missing.

During eruption, permanent teeth may temporarily look crooked (the “ugly duckling” stage). However, if it’s pronounced, early Orthodontics (Phase 1) may be needed, so we recommend an Orthodontic consultation around age 7.

TreatmentPriceNotes
