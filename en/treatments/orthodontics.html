Seoul BD Dental Bracket orthodontic system

We use only two proven premium orthodontic appliances

Self-ligating

Clippy-C

Self-ligating ceramic brackets by TOMY. The clip automatically secures the wire, so that reduced friction + faster tooth movementare achieved.

  • Self-ligating — automatic clip holds the wire
  • Low friction — less discomfort, faster movement
  • Longer intervals between visits — every 6~8 weeks
  • Tooth-colored ceramic — aesthetic
  • Shorter treatment time possible
Recommended for: Fast treatment, less discomfort, fewer visits
Learn more about Clippy-C
Premium esthetics

Clarity Ultra

3M’s top-tier ceramic bracket. With APC Flash-Free bonding technology, clear ceramic with virtually no discolorationis achieved.

  • 3M premium — top-tier ceramic
  • High translucency — minimal discoloration
  • Flash-Free — clean bonding
  • Strong bond strength — Minimizes detachment
  • Smooth contours — Less irritation
Recommended: Highest aesthetics, no discoloration concerns
Learn more about 클라리티울트라

Treatment Journey Guide

Orthodontics Treatment Process

A structured 6-step process from consultation to retention

Consultation Planning Bonding Adjustments Removal Retention
1

Detailed consultation & exam

3D CT, plus an intraoral scanner, bite analysis, and other digital precision exams to accurately assess your current teeth and jaw structure.

About 40 minutes Painless 4th-floor Orthodontic Center
We show you a before-and-after orthodontic simulation video. "My teeth will change like this?" See it for yourself!
2

Create your orthodontic plan

Based on your exam results, we select the optimal appliance between 클리피씨/클라리티울트라 and clearly explain the expected timeline and cost.

About 20 minutes Cost estimate included Consultation with an orthodontic specialist
Please ask any questions. We’ll explain the appliance options, timeline, and costs in detail.
3

Appliance placement

On the tooth surface ceramic bracketsare precisely bonded. They are similar to your tooth color, so they’re not very noticeable.

About 1 hour Mild foreign-body sensation Esthetic ceramic
Your mouth may feel awkward for the first 3~5 days, but you’ll adapt quickly. Eat soft foods.
4

Regular visits & wire adjustments

We replace the wire at intervals of 6~8 weeks for 클리피씨 and 4~6 weeks for 클라리티울트라 to move teeth precisely.

About 20 minutes per visit Every 4~8 weeks Sore for 1~2 days after adjustment
You may feel soreness for 1~2 days after a wire change. It’s a good sign that your teeth are moving!
5

Appliance removal

When we reach your orthodontic goals, we remove the brackets. We finish by cleaning the tooth surfaces—enjoy your new smile!

About 30~40 minutes Painless See your new smile!
The moment your efforts pay off! We place your retainer right after removal.
6

Retainer & care

Wear your retainer so your teeth don’t drift back after orthodontics. Regular checkupsto maintain a beautiful smile.

Retainer wear 6 months~1 year Painless Maintain a beautiful smile
Orthodontic treatment complete! Wear your retainer as directed to keep a beautiful smile for life.

Total orthodontic treatment time About 1 year 6 months~2 years 6 months (varies by case)

Cheonan Orthodontic Pricing

“How much does orthodontic treatment cost in Cheonan?” — We disclose Seoul BD Dental’s orthodontic fees as they are. Depending on the appliance type and treatment scope, the fee is 3,000,000–7,000,000 KRW (approx. $2,190–$5,110).
