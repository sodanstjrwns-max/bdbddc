We use only two clinically proven premium orthodontic appliances

Self-ligating

Clippy-C

Self-ligating ceramic brackets by TOMY. The clip automatically secures the wire Minimized friction + faster tooth movementare achieved.

  • Self-ligating — Wire automatically clipped in place
  • Low friction — Less discomfort, faster movement
  • Longer intervals between visits — 6~8 weeks apart
  • Tooth-colored ceramic — Aesthetic
  • Shorter treatment time possible
Recommended for: Faster treatment, less discomfort, fewer visits
Learn more about Clippy-C
Premium aesthetics

Clarity Ultra

3M's top-tier ceramic brackets. With APC Flash-Free adhesive technology clear ceramic that hardly discolorsis achieved.

  • 3M premium — Top-tier ceramic
  • High transparency — Hardly any discoloration
  • Flash-Free — Clean bonding
  • Strong bond strength — Minimizes debonding
  • Smooth contours — Less irritation
Recommended: Highest esthetics, no discoloration.

Treatment Journey Guide

Orthodontics Treatment Process

A structured 6-step process from consultation to retention

Consultation Planning Attachment Adjustment Removal Retention
1

Detailed Consultation & Exam

3D CT, plus an intraoral scanner, occlusion analysis, and other digital precision tests to accurately evaluate your current teeth and jaw structure.

About 40 minutes Painless 4th-floor Orthodontic Center
We’ll show you a before-and-after orthodontic simulation video. "My teeth will change like this?" See for yourself!
2

Orthodontic Treatment Planning

Based on your exam results, we choose the optimal appliance between 클리피씨/클라리티울트라 and clearly explain the expected duration and cost.

About 20 minutes Includes cost estimate Orthodontist consultation
Please feel free to ask any questions. We’ll explain appliance types, treatment time, and costs in detail.
3

Appliance Placement

On the tooth surface ceramic brackets are precisely bonded. Because they match your tooth color, they’re not easily noticeable.

About 1 hour Slight sensation of something foreign Esthetic ceramic
Your mouth may feel awkward for the first 3~5 days, but you’ll adapt quickly. Stick to soft foods.
4

Regular visits & wire adjustments

With 클리피씨 every 6~8 weeks and 클라리티울트라 every 4~6 weeks, we replace the wire to move your teeth precisely.

About 20 minutes per visit 4~8-week intervals Soreness for 1~2 days after adjustments
You may feel sore for 1~2 days after a wire change. That’s a good sign your teeth are moving!
5

Appliance Removal

When we reach the planned results, we remove the brackets. We finish by cleaning the tooth surfaces so you can see your new smile!

About 30~40 minutes Painless See your new smile!
The moment your hard work pays off! Right after removal, we place your retainer.
6

Retainer & Care

Wear your retainer after treatment to keep your teeth from shifting back. Regular checkups help you maintain a beautiful smile.

Retainer: 6 months–1 year Painless Maintain a beautiful smile
Orthodontic treatment complete! If you wear your retainer properly, you can maintain a beautiful smile for life.

Total orthodontic treatment time Approximately 1 year 6 months–2 years 6 months (varies by case)

It depends on the case. For mild to moderate cases, Invisalign (clear aligners) works well, while for complex cases (severe crowding, protrusion, extraction cases), bracket braces can deliver more precise results. We’ll determine the best option together after a specialist consultation.

Crooked teeth affect not only appearance but also chewing function, speech, TMJ (Jaw Joint) health, and oral hygiene. Orthodontic treatment can create aligned teeth, proper bite, and a confident smile, and it helps protect your long‑term dental health.
