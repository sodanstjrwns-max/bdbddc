What is Roxolid SLActive?

Roxolid® is a high-performance alloy developed by Straumann specifically for dental implants, 85% titanium + 15% zirconium composition. Compared with pure titanium, it provides higher mechanical strength and excellent osseointegration ability. SLActive® is a chemically modified ultra-hydrophilic surface technology that achieves faster, more predictable osseointegration than conventional SLA.

Two core technologies in synergy

The perfect combination of material (Roxolid®) and surface (SLActive®)

Roxolid® — high-performance alloy

  • An alloy for dental implants composed of 85% titanium + 15% zirconium
  • Higher tensile strength than pure titanium (excellent mechanical strength)
  • Allows placement of narrow-diameter (2.9mm BLT) implants — maximizes bone preservation
  • Applicable to short-length (4mm SP Short) implants — minimizes bone grafting
  • Offers more treatment options and enables less invasive surgery
  • Validated by numerous clinical studies, including follow-ups of 5 years or longer

SLActive® — ultra-hydrophilic surface

  • Chemically modified SLA surface — achieves ultra-hydrophilicity (super-hydrophilic)
  • Immediately binds with blood to promote fibrin network formation
  • Accelerated osseointegration vs. conventional SLA — 6~8 weeks
  • Promotes osteoblast differentiation and attachment
  • Average bone loss of 0.5~1mm at 10 years — proven long-term stability
  • Excellent outcomes even in patients who received radiation therapy (Nelson K et al., 2016)

Material, surface, and clinical evidence—the three essentials are all outstanding

Who is this recommendedfor

Cases where Straumann Roxolid SLActive is ideal

Those with narrow ridge width

The ultra-small 2.9mm diameter allows placement without bone grafting.

Those who want to minimize bone grafting

Thanks to the high strength of the Roxolid® alloy, even short 4mm implants are possible, reducing vertical bone grafting.

Those who want faster osseointegration

With the SLActive® surface, osseointegration is achieved within 6~8 weeks—about half the time of conventional surfaces (12~16 weeks).

Patients with systemic conditions

High success rates have been clinically demonstrated even in patients with diabetes, prior radiation therapy, or immunosuppression.

Those who want top-tier quality

40-year history, the most clinical papers worldwide, and a lifetime (conditional) warranty—ideal for those who value premium quality.

Those for whom long-term stability matters

Bone loss 0.5~1mm at 10 years; peri-implantitis 1.8%. The best choice if you want an implant that will last.
