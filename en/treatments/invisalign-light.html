What is Invisalign Lite?

Invisalign Lite is a package that efficiently corrects mild malocclusion—in a short time. It includes 14 stages of aligners and one refinement. It’s suitable for simpler cases such as mild front-teeth alignment, minor space closure, or relapse after previous orthodontic treatment (retreatment), and features a short 6–12 month treatment period.
