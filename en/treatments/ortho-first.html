What is First-Phase Orthodontics?

First-Phase Orthodontics is a Phase 1 program designed for growing children ages 6~10. It begins during the mixed dentition stage, when baby and permanent teeth are both present, to guide proper eruption of permanent teeth, widen a narrow dental arch, and balance upper–lower jaw growth. Starting early during growth can greatly shorten and simplify Phase 2 (full) orthodontic treatment.

A structured 6-step treatment process

From consultation to retention

1

Growth check & consultation

3D CT, an intraoral scanner, and growth analysis to assess your child’s oral development.

2

Set the Phase 1 treatment plan

We create a customized growth-phase plan, including guiding permanent-tooth eruption, arch expansion, and more.

3

Appliance placement

We place an orthodontic appliance tailored to your child’s mouth.

4

Regular visits & progress monitoring

Visit every 4–8 weeks to check growth and adjust the appliance.

5

Complete Phase 1 & monitor progress

After achieving Phase 1 goals, we monitor the transition to permanent teeth.

6

Evaluate Phase 2 orthodontics

After all permanent teeth have erupted, we determine whether comprehensive Phase 2 orthodontics is needed.
