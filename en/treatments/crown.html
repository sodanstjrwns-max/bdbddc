CrownWhat is it?

A crown is an artificial restoration that encases the entire tooth when it’s severely damaged. Zirconia, ceramic, and similar materials, it restores both function and appearance.

Root Canal Treatment after, or when a tooth is badly damaged, we place a Zirconia or all-ceramic crown to completely encase and protect it.

Do you have any of these concerns on your mind?

Many people have the same concerns

"After Root Canal Treatment, is a crown really necessary?" Yes, Tooth fracture prevention is essential
"I don’t like the look of metal crowns" Tooth-colored Zirconia/Ceramic
"Can a crown come off?" Long-term retention with precision bonding

Crown Material comparison

The optimal material for your tooth’s condition and location

Zirconia

A premium material that offers both strength and esthetics. Suitable for front teeth andmolars alike.

Highest strengthEstheticVersatile material

All-ceramic (E.max)

Replicates translucency and shade closest to natural teeth. Especially suitable for front teeth.

Superior estheticsNatural translucencyRecommended for front teeth

PFM (porcelain-fused-to-metal)

A traditional crown with porcelain fired over a metal base. Covered by Korean NHIS.

4

Final crown placement

After confirming fit and shade, we bond precisely and adjust your bite.

A crown is a prosthetic cap that covers an entire tooth damaged by a cavity or fracture. It restores the tooth’s strength, shape, and function. It is commonly recommended after a Root Canal Treatment, treatment of a large cavity, or a tooth fracture.

① PFM (porcelain-fused-to-metal crown) — metal inside + porcelain outside ② All-ceramic (e.max) — most similar to natural teeth ③ Zirconia — strongest and esthetic ④ Gold crown — best biocompatibility. We recommend the optimal material based on the tooth’s position and intended use.
