What is a Navigation-Guided Implant?

A Navigation-Guided Implant is an advanced digital surgery in which, based on 3D CT imaging, a computer tracks the instrument's position, angle, and depth in real time to place the implant precisely at the planned site.

Navigation-guided treatment journey

0.1mm precision placement, 6 steps process

Real-time 3D tracking enables the safest placement

CT Analysis Simulation Guide Placement Osseointegration Prosthetics
1

High-precision 3D CT analysis

3D CTIt analyzes jawbone density, inferior alveolar nerve location, and sinus distance at 0.1mm resolution. This is the starting point for navigation-guided placement.

About 20 minutes Painless 5th-floor Screening Center
A more precise CT analysis is performed than with standard Dental Implants. We precisely map the 3D positions of nerves and blood vessels.
2

Virtual surgical simulation

Based on CT data, we simulate placement position, angle, and depth on the computer. We predict the outcome in advance to determine optimal positioning before surgery.

About 30 minutes Painless 3D simulation
We show you the simulation results directly. Visually confirm: "We will place it here at this angle."
3

Surgical guide Fabrication

We fabricate a patient-specific surgical guide based on the simulation data. This guide links with the navigation system to track position in real time.

Fabrication about 3~5 days Custom-made 14-specialist team finalized
At this step, our team of 14 specialists finalizes the surgical plan. Please ask any questions you may have.
4

Real-time navigation-guided placement

With a real-time 3D position-tracking system, we place the implant with accuracy within 0.1mm. The implant position is displayed on the monitor in real time, allowing us to safely avoid nerves and blood vessels.

About 15~25 minutes per implant Painless (anesthesia/sedation) 6 independent operating rooms
Surgery time reduced by 30~50% compared to conventional Dental Implants! Because placement follows the simulation precisely, errors are minimal.
5

Osseointegration & Follow-up

Precise placement leads to a high osseointegration success rate. With SLActive, this period can be shortened to 6~8 weeks.

General 2~4 months / SLActive 6~8 weeks Able to continue daily activities
Precise placement minimizes bone damage, resulting in a higher osseointegration success rate than conventional Dental Implants.
6

Custom prosthesis completed

At our in-house lab, using precise placement data, we fabricate a custom Zirconia CrownWe fabricate and place it. Esthetics are indistinguishable from natural teeth.

About 30 minutes (2 visits) Painless Natural-tooth-level esthetics
Treatment complete! Precise placement supports a high long-term success rate. We recommend checkups every 6 months.

Total treatment duration About 3~6 months, 4~6 visits
