What is Scaling (Dental Cleaning)?

Scaling is an oral care procedure that removes tartar and plaque built up on tooth surfaces using an ultrasonic device. For those aged 19 and older, it is covered by Korean NHIS once per year and is the most basic treatment for preventing gum disease.

Ultrasonic scaling removes tartar and plaque to protect your gums. It’s covered by Korean NHIS, so your out-of-pocket cost is minimal.
