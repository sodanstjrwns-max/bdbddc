Gum (Periodontal) Treatment

Periodontitis,
Managed systematically

What is periodontal disease?

Periodontal disease is inflammation of the tissues that support the gums and teeth (periodontal ligament, and alveolar bone) become inflamed. Early gingivitiscan progress to periodontitis, causing teeth to loosen or be lost, so early treatment is important.

Periodontitis is a serious disease that destroys the alveolar bone. With early detection and systematic treatment, we preserve your teeth.

Periodontitis warning signs

If you have these symptoms, please get checked right away.

Bleeding gums

Brushingor eating can make your gums bleed.

Severe Bad breath

Bad breath persists even after brushing.

Loose teeth

Teeth become loose or shift position.

Gum recession

The gums recede, making teeth look longer.

Periodontitis Treatment process

Structured, step-by-step care

1

Comprehensive periodontal exam

Periodontal pocket We diagnose accurately with pocket measurement, X-rays, and bacterial testing.

2

Non-surgical treatment

Scaling (Dental Cleaning), and root planing to remove tartar and infected tissue.

3

Surgical treatment (if needed)

For moderate or more severe cases Gum surgery, Bone Graft (GBR) and other surgical treatments as needed.

4

Maintenance care

Regular care every 3~4 months helps prevent recurrence.

Periodontitis (gum disease) treatment cost guide — covered by Korean NHIS

"How much does periodontitis treatment cost?" — Periodontal care is covered by Korean NHIS, and your out-of-pocket cost per visit is in the range of a few thousand to tens of thousands KRW.
