Root Canal Treatment (Endodontic) Frequently Asked Questions 20 items

Root Canal Treatment (endodontic therapy) is performed when the tooth’s internal nerve (pulp) is infected or necrotic due to a Cavity or trauma; the infected tissue is removed, the inside is disinfected, and the canals are filled. It is an important way to preserve a tooth without extracting it.
With adequate anesthesia, there is little to no pain during treatment. In fact, the toothache before a root canal is usually much worse. You may feel mild discomfort for 1~2 days afterward, which can be controlled with pain relievers.
Once the nerve is removed, blood supply to the tooth stops, so it can become dry and more fragile. For this reason, it is standard to place a Crown (restoration) after Root Canal Treatment to protect the tooth.
Typically, 2~3 visits are needed. Depending on the degree of infection, more visits may be required, with 1~2 weeks between appointments. In cases of acute pain, we provide emergency care first and then proceed step by step.
If an infected nerve is left untreated, tooth root at the tip, pus (Periapical abscess) can develop, and the infection may spread to the surrounding bone. This causes facial swelling and severe pain, and extraction can ultimately become unavoidable.
With proper Root Canal Treatment plus protection with a Crown, it can last a long time. However, reinfection, tooth fracture are possible risks, so Routine checkupsare important. The success rate is about 90~95%.
At Seoul BD Dental, digital X-rayand an electronic apex locator enable precise Root Canal Treatment. An endodontic specialist performs the treatment directly, maximizing the chance of preserving the tooth with a minimally invasive approach.
Pain may temporarily worsen between visits. This is a reaction as medicaments used during treatment act on the tissues, and it can be managed with pain relievers. However, if significant swelling or fever occurs, please visit us immediately.
In most cases, we recommend covering the tooth with a Crown (restoration). After Root Canal Treatment, the tooth loses its moisture supply, becomes dry, and is more prone to breaking. Especially molarshave strong biting forces, so using them without a Crown increases the risk of fracture.
As time passes after Root Canal Treatment, the tooth can become darker. This occurs as residual blood components break down. internal whiteningcan be improved with (walking bleach), and for front teeth Laminate Veneeror a Crown can restore esthetics.

If inflammation at the root tip (periapical lesion) remains, Root Canal Retreatment or perform apical surgery (Apicoectomy). Success rates: initial treatment about 90-95%, retreatment about 70-85%.
Root Canal Treatment is covered by Korean NHIS, and your out-of-pocket portion is about 30,000-100,000 KRW (approx. $22-$73), depending on tooth position and number of roots. However, the Crown placed afterward may be non-covered (out-of-pocket) depending on the material, so we will explain the total cost during your consultation.
During treatment (when you have a temporary filling), avoid chewing hard foods on that tooth. The temporary filling may come out, and the weakened tooth can fracture. Chew on the opposite side, and if the temporary filling comes out, please visit us right away.
When infection recurs in a previously treated tooth, we remove the old filling materials and disinfect and fill the canals again. It is more complex than the initial treatment, but often the last chance to avoid extraction and save the tooth.
When standard Root Canal Treatment or Root Canal Retreatment cannot solve the problem, we open the gum and remove the infected root tip.dental operating microscope under which we perform with high precision, and as the last Conservative Treatment approach.
