Most Frequently Asked Questions

Top 3 most-asked questions from patients

1

My tooth suddenly hurts so much—what should I do?

Take an over-the-counter pain reliever (ibuprofen-based) and apply a cold compress to the painful area.

2

I broke a tooth—what should I do?

Find the broken piece, store it in milk or saline, and visit a dentist immediately.

3

My tooth was completely knocked out!

Pick up the tooth without touching the root, store it in milk (or keep it in your mouth if no milk), then visit a dentist within 30 minutes.
