Invisalign FAQs 24

Invisalign uses clear plastic aligners for orthodontic correction. Unlike traditional wire braces, they are barely noticeable and can be removed for eating and brushing. Chosen by over 17 million people worldwide, it is the most widely used clear aligner system.
It varies by scope (partial/full), complexity, and treatment duration. At Seoul BD Dental, we provide an exact quote after a free consultation, with interest-free payment plans and card payment available.
Typically 12~18 months, and simple limited corrections can be done within 6 months. With ClinCheck (3D simulation), you can preview the expected timeline and results before starting treatment.
Wearing them 20~22 hours a day is recommended. Remove them only for meals and brushing; keeping them in the rest of the time keeps treatment on schedule. If wear time is insufficient, your treatment may take longer.
You may feel mild pressure for 1~3 days after switching to a new set, but discomfort is far less than with braces. Most people adapt without interference in daily life, and pain relievers are rarely necessary.
From mild crowding to moderate malocclusion malocclusionare mostly treatable. However, severe skeletal malocclusion or complex cases may be better suited to traditional braces. A precise plan is decided after an orthodontist’s evaluation.
Clean them daily with lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush. Avoid hot water, which can cause deformation. Using a dedicated cleanser allows for even more hygienic care.
Because you remove the aligners to eat, there are no food restrictions—one of the biggest advantages over braces. Just brush after meals and put them back in.
For the first 6 months after finishing treatment, wear it nearly all day; afterwards, only while sleeping. RetainerWear your retainer as directed; neglecting it can lead to relapse, so please follow your orthodontist’s instructions.
Invisalign is clear and discreet, removable, less painful, and makes oral hygiene easier. Traditional braces are effective for complex cases and may be relatively less expensive. At Seoul BD Dental, we recommend the best option for each patient.
Absolutely. Invisalign is especially popular with adults. Working professionals and college students who care about appearance often choose clear aligners, and there’s no age limit—as long as your gums and teeth are healthy, orthodontic treatment is possible.
Seoul BD Dental is an officially certified Invisalign provider with extensive case experience. With 3D ClinCheck simulation, you can preview results before treatment, and an orthodontist manages your care from start to finish.
At Seoul BD Dental, instead of the conventional, uncomfortable impressions(impressions) iTero a digital scanner. In just 2~3 minutes without gagging, a precise 3D model of your teeth is created, and Invisalign fabricates your custom aligners based on it.

If you lose one, contact the clinic immediately. You may go back to the previous set or move to the next set. Sometimes a remake is needed, so we recommend keeping the previous set.

Invisalign itself does not affect pregnancy. However, hormonal changes during pregnancy can make your gums more sensitive, so take extra care with oral hygiene. During the first 1~2 weeks, you may notice a slight lisp, but most people adapt quickly. For important situations like presentations or interviews, you can temporarily remove the aligners—another advantage of Invisalign.
