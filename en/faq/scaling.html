Scaling (Dental Cleaning) Frequently Asked Questions 19

Scaling (Dental Cleaning) is a procedure that uses an ultrasonic instrument to remove tartar (calcified plaque) built up on tooth surfaces and between the teeth and gums. Gum disease It is the most basic and important step for prevention and treatment.
For patients aged 19 and older, it is covered once per year by Korean NHIS (resets on January 1 each year). The patient copay is about 15,000–20,000 KRW (approx. $11–$15). More than once per year or additional periodontal treatment may be non-covered (out-of-pocket).
If you have heavy tartar or your gums are not in good health, you may feel some discomfort during the procedure. If the pain is significant, we can numb the area first. With regular cleanings, less tartar builds up and discomfort decreases.
This is normal; tartar had been filling the spaces between your teeth, and once it is removed you may notice gaps. As your gums heal and become healthier over time, this sensation resolves naturally. Leaving tartar in place will worsen gum disease.

In general, once every 6 months is standard, but it depends on your gum condition. With healthy gums, once a year is fine; if you have gum disease or tartar builds up quickly, we recommend every 3~4 months. Korean NHIS coverage applies once per year.
Scaling (Dental Cleaning) removes tartar and surface stains (coffee and tobacco), so your teeth can return to their natural color. However, this is different from Teeth Whitening, which lightens the tooth itself. The clean feeling after scaling comes from stain removal.

Scaling (Dental Cleaning) removes tartar above the gumline (supragingival), while SRP (Scaling and Root Planing) is a deep cleaning that removes tartar and infected tissue below the gumline (subgingival). Scaling is preventive; SRP is therapeutic and performed under local anesthesia.

Electric toothbrushcan remove plaque more effectively than a manual toothbrush, but tartar (calcified plaque) cannot be removed at home. Tartar must be removed with professional dental instruments, so regular Scaling (Dental Cleaning) is necessary regardless of electric toothbrush use.
Anyone aged 19 or older can receive Scaling (Dental Cleaning) covered by Korean NHIS. Tartar builds up regardless of age, so starting regular cleanings early is the best way to protect gum health. In particular, smokers and orthodontic patients should have it more often.
