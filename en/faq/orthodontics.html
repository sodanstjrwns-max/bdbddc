Most Frequently Asked Questions

Top 3 most-asked by patients

1

Why is orthodontic treatment necessary?

Crooked teeth affect not only appearance but also chewing function, speech, TMJ (jaw joint) health, and oral hygiene.

2

What types of orthodontic treatment are available?

Metal braces (most economical), Ceramic Orthodontics (tooth-colored brackets), self-ligating braces (Clippy-C, Damon), Lingual braces(attached on the inside), Invisalign(clear aligners) are available.

3

How much does orthodontic treatment cost?

It varies by appliance type, case complexity, and treatment duration.

Crooked teeth affect more than appearance—they can impact chewing function, speech, TMJ (Jaw Joint) health, and oral hygiene. Orthodontic treatment can create well-aligned teeth, a proper bite, and a confident smile, helping protect your long-term oral health.
Metal braces (most economical), ceramic braces (tooth-colored brackets), self-ligating braces (Clippy-C, Damon), lingual braces (attached on the inside), and Invisalign (clear aligners) are available. Each has pros and cons, so we recommend deciding after a consultation with an orthodontist.
It depends on the appliance type, complexity, and duration. Metal braces are the most economical; Invisalign or lingual braces tend to cost more. At Seoul BD Dental, we provide an exact estimate after a free consultation.
On average, 1 year 6 months–2 years 6 months; partial treatment is about 6 months–1 year. Depending on the method, Malocclusionthe severity of the malocclusion and your level of cooperation.
For 3–5 days after the appliance is placed, you may feel a dull ache as the teeth begin to move, but it is tolerable. After wire adjustments, mild discomfort can last another 2–3 days and then gradually subsides as you adapt.
With fixed braces (metal, ceramic), avoid hard foods (candy, ice) and sticky foods (caramel, tteok). With clear aligners (Invisalign), you remove the trays to eat, so there are no dietary restrictions.
Use an orthodontic toothbrush, interdental brush, and a Waterpik to thoroughly clean around the appliances. Ideally, brush after every meal, oral irrigator is recommended. Inadequate care can lead to cavities or gum disease.

We can temporarily adjust or remove the orthodontic appliance and then proceed with cavity treatment. During orthodontic care, food can get trapped easily and the risk of cavities increases, so meticulous home care with an orthodontic toothbrush, interdental brush, and Waterpik is important.
Self-ligating systems use brackets with built-in clips to secure the wire, so rubber ligatures are not needed. With less friction, tooth movement can be more efficient, discomfort may be reduced, and visit intervals can often be extended.
Lingual braces are attached to the inside surfaces of the teeth, so they are not visible from the outside. They are popular with people in appearance-sensitive jobs (broadcasters, flight attendants, sales, etc.) and those preparing for a wedding. However, an adaptation period for speech is needed, and the cost tends to be higher.

Orthodontic appliances (especially metal·ceramic brackets) may make your lips look slightly more prominent due to its thickness. This is temporary and will return to normal once the appliance is removed. In fact, after orthodontic treatment is completed and the teeth are well aligned, your lip profile often improves.
Clear alignersClear aligners (Invisalign) are esthetic and convenient, but for complex cases braces may be more effective. Mild to moderate malocclusion suits clear aligners, while severe malocclusion, extraction cases, or skeletal issues favor braces. The final plan is determined after an orthodontic specialist’s evaluation.
Orthodontic miniscrew is used as anchorage by placing a small screw in the jawbone to move teeth. It is effective for extraction cases or Impacted tooth during traction. It is placed easily under local anesthesia. After orthodontic treatment is complete, it is easily removed and leaves no scar.
The American Association of Orthodontists recommends the first orthodontic consultation at age 7. Phase 1 (early/interceptive) treatment is at ages 7–10, Phase 2 treatment(comprehensive treatment) is permanent teethare fully in (12–14 years). Jaw growth problems are most effectively treated during the growth period.
Orthodontic alignment affects the teeth and lip profile; when correcting a protrusive profile, the lips may retract and the E-line can improve, changing your side profile. However, it does not change the jawbone itself, so severe skeletal problems may require double-jaw (orthognathic) surgery.
Using modern self-ligating brackets, micro-vibration devices (AcceleDent), or corticotomy (micro cortical bone cuts) can increase the rate of tooth movement. Keeping every appointment and carefully following your orthodontist’s instructions can also shorten overall treatment time.
