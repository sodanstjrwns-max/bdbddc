Glownate (Laminate Veneer) Frequently Asked Questions 24

Glownate is Seoul BD Dental’s premium Laminate Veneer brand. A thin CeramicA cosmetic treatment that bonds a thin shell to improve the color, shape, and size of your teeth. It’s a popular choice among celebrities and influencers.
Glownate is Seoul BD Dental’s premium Laminate Veneer system that uses minimal‑prep techniques and a custom shade system. Compared with conventional laminates, it looks more natural and causes less tooth reduction.
Laminate Veneer costs vary by material type (e.max, Zirconia etc.) and by the number of teeth treated. At Seoul BD Dental, we provide an exact quote after a consultation, and interest‑free installments are available.
Typically it’s completed in 2~3 visits over about 1~2 weeks. First visit: tooth preparation + temporary veneers. Second visit: final veneer bonding.
Glownate follows a minimal‑prep principle. Typically only about 0.3~0.5mm is reduced, and depending on the case, no‑prep Laminate Veneer may be possible. We enhance esthetics while preserving your tooth health as much as possible.
With good care, they can last 10~15 years or more. Regular checkups and proper oral hygiene, while avoiding excessive force (teeth grinding, hard foods, etc.), can help extend their lifespan.
Tooth preparation Local anesthesia is used during this step, so there is little to no pain during treatment. Mild sensitivity may occur for 1~2 days afterward, but it resolves soon.
Unlike natural teeth, ceramic veneers do not discolor. However, the bonding margins or adjacent natural teeth can stain, so brushing after coffee or wine is recommended.
Composite Resin is applied directly to the tooth and is more affordable, but it discolors easily and has a shorter lifespan (3~5 years). Laminate Veneer is ceramic, resists discoloration, lasts longer (10~15 years), and provides a more natural result.
Brush just like your natural teeth. However, avoid biting hard foods (raw carrots, ice, etc.) with your front teeth, and Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) If you have this, we recommend wearing a night guard. Get a checkup every 6 months.
Glownate at Seoul BD Dental delivers natural‑looking results through a custom shade system, minimal‑prep techniques, and the meticulous care of our cosmetic dentistry specialist. You can preview the outcome in advance with a mock‑up (simulation).
Yes, they can. A gap between the front teeth (spacing, diastema) is a classic indication for Laminate Veneers. By designing the tooth width slightly wider, we can close the space naturally.
Yes—Laminate Veneers are effective even when changing only the shade. They are an excellent solution for severe discoloration that does not respond to whitening, such as tetracycline staining or fluorosis spots. With Glownate’s custom shade system, we can precisely achieve your desired brightness.
Everyday meals are fine, but avoid hard foods (biting into a whole apple, crunching ice, etc.). Ceramic is strong under compression but can be more vulnerable to twisting or impact. If you have teeth grinding (bruxism), wear a night guard at night.
Visit a dentist immediately. If you bring the veneer that came off, it may be possible to rebond it. If rebonding isn’t possible, a new one will be made. At Seoul BD Dental, we provide a quick temporary fix and proceed with a remake.
Usually, treating the 6~8 upper front teeth visible in your smile (canine to canine) looks the most natural. If you do only 1~2, there may be color and shape differences with adjacent teeth, so designing for overall harmony is important.
You may feel minor discomfort along the gumline right after the procedure, but it typically subsides within 1~2 days. Precisely fabricated Laminate Veneers do not irritate the gums. If gum discomfort persists, please visit us to check the fit.
Laminate Veneers involve removing only about 0.3~0.5mm from the front surface and bonding a thin ceramic, Crownremoves about 1~2mm from the entire tooth and covers it. While Laminate Veneers are more conservative for tooth preservation, a Crown is appropriate when there is significant decay or a large fracture.
A mock-up is a process that lets you preview the expected result using temporary material before the actual Laminate Veneer procedure. You can check tooth length, shape, and color in advance, and we finalize the design by incorporating your preferences. At Glownate, this step is essential.
A no-prep Laminate Veneer bonds an ultra-thin ceramic (0.1~0.2mm) without any tooth reduction. It’s suitable when teeth are small or spaced, and its biggest advantage is avoiding any tooth damage. However, it is not applicable to every case.
It’s extremely rare, but excessive reduction can affect the nerve. Glownate strictly follows a minimal-reduction principle (0.3~0.5mm) to minimize this risk. Before treatment, we perform precise evaluations to confirm tooth thickness and nerve position.
Most celebrities’ tooth reshapinginvolves Laminate Veneers or all-ceramic Crowns. Glownate aims for results so natural they’re hard to distinguish from real teeth, with a core philosophy of 'undetectable beauty.'
The veneers themselves do not respond to whitening agents, so their color will not change. However, whitening the neighboring natural teeth can create a color mismatch. If you want whitening, do it before your Laminate Veneers, or select the veneer shade to match after whitening.
