Dental Implant FAQs 25

A dental implant is a treatment that places a titanium artificial screw in the jawbone (alveolar bone) at the site of a missing tooth and connects an artificial tooth (Crown) on top. It restores function and esthetics most similar to natural teeth and is widely recognized as the best treatment for tooth loss today.
The cost of a dental implant varies by the fixture (screw) brand used, the type of restoration on top, and whether bone grafting is needed. At Seoul BD Dental, we provide an exact estimate after consultation, and interest-free installments are available. For patients aged 65 and older, the procedure can be covered by Korean NHIS (30% patient co-pay).
The implant itself (titanium fixture) is semi-permanent and, with good care, can last 20~30 years or more. However, the upper Crown may need replacement every 10~15 years. Regular checkupsand proper oral care are key to longevity.
With adequate local anesthesia during the procedure, there is little to no pain. At Seoul BD Dental, implants can also be performed under sedation, dental phobiacan still receive treatment comfortably. Mild swelling and discomfort for 2~3 days after the procedure are possible, but they are well controlled with prescribed medication.
Typically, the mandible (lower jaw) 2~3 months; maxilla (upper jaw) about 4~6 months. If bone grafting is needed, additional time may be required. At Seoul BD Dental, same-day implants (immediate placement) is also possible, and depending on your condition, the optimal plan is established.
Most adults are candidates for implants; however, those with uncontrolled diabetes, severe osteoporosis, or undergoing cancer treatment require prior evaluation. Even when the jawbone is insufficient, implants are possible with bone grafting (GBR) can make implants feasible. An accurate assessment is made after a CT scan and consultation with a specialist.
For those aged 65 and older, up to two implants per lifetime are covered by Korean NHIS. The patient co-pay is about 30% (about 500,000–600,000 KRW), and for the second-lowest income bracket the co-pay is 10%. For patients under 65, implants are non-covered (out-of-pocket); we will provide exact fees during consultation.
On the day of the procedure, it’s best to eat soft foods (porridge, soup, etc.) on the opposite side. You can usually return to a regular diet after about 1~2 weeks, and after the final prosthesis is placed you can eat almost all foods. However, hard items like ice or bones are best avoided even with natural teeth.
Brush 2~3 times a day, just like your natural teeth Brushing + Using an interdental brush or dental floss is essential. Peri-implantitis(Dental Implant gum disease) To prevent this, have regular checkups every 3~6 months and Scaling (Dental Cleaning)is important.
Modern Dental Implants have a 95~98% success rate. However, failure can occur due to smoking, poor maintenance, or excessive forces. At Seoul BD Dental, we minimize the risk with CT-based precision diagnosis and collaborative care by specialists trained at Seoul National University.
Yes. For front-tooth implants, esthetics are critical, so the gum line, tooth color, and shape must be precisely planned. At Seoul BD Dental, we use digital scanning and custom abutments to deliver front-tooth implants that are hard to distinguish from natural teeth.
A Bone Graft (GBR) is a procedure to add bone when there isn’t enough to place an implant. It is performed under anesthesia, so there is no pain during the procedure; swelling and discomfort may last about 3~5 days afterward. Bone grafting is an important step that improves implant success.
Dental Bridgehas the drawback of requiring reduction of the two healthy neighboring teeth, whereas a Dental Implant is placed independently without damaging adjacent teeth. In the long term, a Dental Implant excels in preserving adjacent teeth, Bone loss (resorption) prevention and ease of cleaning. However, depending on your overall health, a Dental Bridge may be appropriate, so we recommend consulting a specialist.
Seoul BD Dental has 6 independent operating rooms and operates an air-shower infection-control system. A team of 14 specialists trained at Seoul National University co-treat cases, and we establish optimal treatment plans with CT- and digital scan–based precision diagnostics. With care available 365 days a year, even busy professionals can visit conveniently.
Navigation-Guided Implantuses CT data to fabricate a 3D guide, allowing the implant to be placed at the computer-planned precise position, angle, and depth. Compared with the conventional approach, accuracy is higher, lowering the risk of nerve injury, and minimizing incisions for faster recovery. At Seoul BD Dental, we use Navigation-Guided Implants for complex cases.
Yes, when conditions are right, immediate placement at the time of extraction is possible. If bone is adequate and there is no infection, same-day placement can greatly shorten the treatment period. However, it doesn’t apply to every case; a specialist will determine this after a CT exam.
Avoid strenuous exercise (running, weights, swimming, etc.) for 1~2 weeks after the procedure. Increases in blood pressure can worsen bleeding and swelling. Light walking is fine from the next day, and you can gradually increase intensity after your specialist clears you.
Smoking is Dental Implant failure is one of the biggest risk factors. Nicotine constricts blood vessels and interferes with the bonding of bone and the Dental Implant (). We strongly recommend quitting for at least 4~8 weeks before and after the procedure; complete cessation is ideal if possible.
A natural tooth has periodontal ligament (which acts like a cushion), but a Dental Implant does not, so it attaches directly to the bone. As a result, chewing sensation may feel slightly different, and excessive force is transmitted straight to the bone. Function and esthetics are nearly the same as with natural teeth, but regular maintenance is even more important.
If your diabetes is controlled (HbA1c 7% or below), a Dental Implant is possible. If blood sugar is not well controlled, infection risk increases and osseointegration may be delayed, so we coordinate with your internist to stabilize glucose before treatment. At Seoul BD Dental, we follow a tailored protocol for patients with diabetes.
Peri-implantitis is an inflammatory disease affecting the gums and bone around a Dental Implant, similar to gum disease in natural teeth. The main causes are poor oral hygiene and skipping regular checkups. If detected early, it is treatable, but if neglected, the Dental Implant may need to be removed—so a checkup every 6 months is essential.
The basic principle is the same, but for front-tooth Dental Implants, esthetics (gum line, shade, translucency) are very important, whereas molars Dental Implants must prioritize durability to withstand chewing forces. For front teeth, we use a custom abutment and all-Crowns, while for molars we mainly use Zirconia or gold Crown are commonly used.
Yes. Seoul BD Dental offers various payment options, including interest-free installments (credit card) and dental-only medical financing. We can also discuss a personalized payment plan during your consultation so you can receive care without undue burden.
Regular Dental Implants replace teeth one by one, while Implant Overdenture (overdenture) secures the Denture to 2~4 Dental Implants. It suits patients missing all teeth and is more stable than a conventional Denture, while being more economical than a full set of individual Dental Implants.
Seoul BD Dental offers a warranty program for a set period after a Dental Implant procedure. If you keep up with regular checkups, you can receive warranty benefits. Terms and duration vary by implant fixture brand and procedure type; we will explain the details during your consultation.
