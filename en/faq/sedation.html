Patients' TOP 3 Questions

1

What is Sedation Dentistry (sedation)?

Sedation Dentistry uses IV sedatives to place you in a semi-asleep state, then proceeds with dental treatment.

2

Is Sedation Dentistry safe?

Unlike general anesthesia, sedation maintains spontaneous breathing and is very safe.

3

Who is a good candidate for Sedation Dentistry?

Severe dental phobia,pronounced gag reflex, or those needing long appointments (Dental Implant multiple, etc.), patients with disabilities who have difficulty cooperating, and pediatric patients are candidates.

Sedation Dentistry FAQs 21

Sedation Dentistry uses IV sedatives to place you in a semi-asleep state while dental treatment is performed. Unlike general anesthesia, you remain conscious but do not feel anxiety or pain, and when treatment ends you typically feel as if you just woke from a nap.
Unlike general anesthesia, sedation maintains spontaneous breathing and is considered very safe. At Seoul BD Dental, we monitor blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and heart rate in real time with vital monitors, and experienced specialists perform the procedure.
It’s suitable for people with severe dental anxiety, a strong gag reflex, those who need lengthy care (such as multiple Dental Implants), patients with disabilities who have difficulty cooperating, and children.
Sedation fees are charged separately and vary by procedure time and complexity. At Seoul BD Dental, we’ll provide an exact quote during your consultation.
Please fast for at least 6~8 hours before the procedure (no food or water). Let us know in advance about any medications you are taking. On the day of treatment, come with an escort and avoid driving yourself.
It takes about 2~4 hours for the sedative effects to fully wear off. That day, avoid driving, making important decisions, or strenuous exercise, and go home with an escort. You can return to normal activities the next day.
You’ll be in a semi-asleep state with some awareness, but most patients do not remember the procedure. You can still respond to simple instructions (e.g., "open your mouth").
Yes. For pediatric patients with severe dental anxiety or difficulty cooperating, we use Sedation Dentistry. At Seoul BD Dental, \" Pediatric Dentistry is performed safely by a pediatric specialist.
With sedation, you maintain your own breathing and remain partially conscious; with general anesthesia, you are fully unconscious and need assisted ventilation. For most dental care, sedation is sufficient, with faster recovery and fewer side effects than general anesthesia.
Seoul BD Dental has dedicated sedation equipment and a separate operating room, and we prioritize safety with real-time monitoring systems. With the motto 'A dentist even the timid can feel safe with,' we do our best to help anyone who fears the dentist receive care comfortably.
Because sedation maintains spontaneous breathing, the chance of breathing issues is very low. At Seoul BD Dental, we check oxygen saturation in real time and keep full emergency equipment ready to respond immediately if needed.
Sedation fees are currently non-covered (out-of-pocket) item). They are charged in addition to the treatment cost and vary by procedure time and medications used. However, by completing multiple procedures in one visit with sedation, you can reduce the overall treatment period and number of appointments.
Some patients experience mild nausea or dizziness as the sedative wears off. These usually resolve within 30 minutes~1 hour; if you have symptoms, rest sufficiently in the recovery room before going home.
We use well-established medical agents such as propofol, midazolam, and dexmedetomidine. A specialist administers an appropriate dose based on your weight, health status, and procedure time, with real-time monitoring to ensure safety.
It may be possible, but a pre-procedure evaluation is essential. Please share all current medications, and our specialist will determine safety. Uncontrolled hypertension or significant heart disease may limit sedation, and a note from your primary physician may be required.
Sedation can be applied to almost all dental procedures. Dental Implants, Wisdom Tooth Extraction, multiple cavity treatments, Gum surgery, and pediatric care are typical examples. It’s especially efficient for longer appointments, since multiple procedures can be completed in one visit.
Most patients can resume normal activities the next day. You may feel drowsy the day of the procedure, so avoid important tasks or driving and get plenty of rest. While it varies by individual, full recovery usually occurs within 6~8 hours.
Yes. Medically, repeated sedation is considered safe. The medications used for sedation do not accumulate in the body, and allowing a sufficient interval (usually 1~2 weeks) between sessions is fine.
Laughing gas(N2O) is a light, nose-inhaled form of sedation that helps relieve anxiety but does not put you to sleep. IV sedation induces a deeper level of sedation through an intravenous line. Laughing gas is suitable for mild dental anxiety; IV sedation is appropriate for severe anxiety or long procedures.
Most patients remember little or nothing about the procedure. This is called an "anterograde amnesia" effect and is a major benefit for those with significant dental anxiety. It feels as if you simply fell asleep and woke up.
Because sedation reduces the gag reflex, you need to fast for at least 6~8 hours to prevent stomach contents from refluxing into the airway (aspiration). Avoid even water starting 2 hours before the procedure.
