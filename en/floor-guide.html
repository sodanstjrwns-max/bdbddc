5FFloor

Comprehensive Care Center

General Treatment Center

Cavity Treatment Root Canal Treatment Scaling (Dental Cleaning) Gum (Periodontal) Treatment Teeth Whitening Wisdom Tooth Extraction

We provide general dental care including cavity treatment, root canal treatment, gum (periodontal) treatment, teeth whitening, and wisdom tooth extraction. Our restorative and periodontal specialists provide attentive, personalized care for every patient.

4FFloor

Dental Implant Center

Implant Center

Dental Implant Surgery Sedation Implant Surgery Navigation-Guided Implant Bone Graft (GBR) Surgery 6 Surgical Suites 2 Recovery Rooms

Implant surgeries are performed in 6 state-of-the-art surgical suites and 2 recovery rooms. Using a navigation-guided system for precision and sedation for implants, we provide comfortable, safe treatment.

3FFloor

BDX Checkup Center · Pediatric Dentistry Center

BDX Checkup & Pediatric Center

BDX Comprehensive Checkup CT · Panoramic X-ray 3 Pediatric Dentistry Specialists Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas) Sedation Dedicated Pediatric Treatment Rooms

At the BDX Precision Diagnostics Center, we provide a comprehensive exam using CT, panoramic X-rays, an intraoral camera, and more. Three pediatric dental specialists from Seoul National University offer comfortable, child-friendly care.

2FFloor

Digital Dental Lab · Hygiene Management Center

Digital Lab & Hygiene Center

Digital Dental Lab Hygiene Management Center CAD/CAM System Strategic Planning Office Five Dental Technicians

Our large in-house digital lab in the Chungnam region fabricates custom restorations the same day. With rigorous infection control, including an air-shower system, we maintain a safe care environment.

1FFloor

Invisalign Orthodontic Center

Invisalign Orthodontic Center

Invisalign Two Orthodontic Specialists from Seoul National University iTero Intraoral Scanner 3D ClinCheck Large Orthodontic Center

We are a large, dedicated Invisalign center. Two orthodontic specialists from Seoul National University provide precise orthodontic care using the iTero intraoral scanner and 3D simulation (ClinCheck). Sunday hours make it easy for busy professionals to visit.
