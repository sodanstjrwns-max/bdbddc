Dental indemnity insurance Claim guide
What’s covered, what isn’t, and the paperwork

"Does dental care not work with indemnity insurance?" — We hear this in the operatory all the time, and it’s only half true. Some items, like Wisdom Tooth extractions and Root Canal Treatment, are clearly claimable, while others—such as Dental Implants and Composite Resin—are generally not. Seoul BD Dental organized this guide around the points patients most often find confusing.

01Why dental care can be covered by indemnity insurance — 3 key principles

In short, the copay for dental treatments that are 'covered by Korean NHIS'can be claimed under indemnity insurance. The following three conditions are key.

① Enrollment date: the baseline is October 2009 (2009년 10월)

Indemnity health insurance standardization in 2009년 10월 was the turning point when coverage changed significantly. For plans enrolled after standardization, the NHIS-covered treatment copayare included within the coverage scope, but most products issued before then excluded dental treatment itself (some rider exceptions apply).

② Diagnosis: Dental disease codes (K00~K08)

For a claim to be approved, it must not be for simple cosmetic or preventive care, but for treatment of a disease to qualify. Under the Korean Standard Classification of Diseases, the relevant tooth and supporting-structure codes K00~K08(e.g., Cavity K02, pulpitis K04, periodontal disease K05, etc.) apply to eligible treatments.

③ Items: Only treatments covered by Korean NHIS; non-covered (out-of-pocket) are generally excluded

Even for the same dental treatment, Korean NHIS covered items (Wisdom Tooth extraction, Root Canal Treatment, Gum (Periodontal) Treatment, etc.) are claimable only for your co-pay portion. Treatments such as Dental Implant, Composite Resin, Crown, and Orthodontics are non-covered (out-of-pocket) itemsexcluded from coverage under the standard policy terms.

Quick summary

Enrolled after 2009년 10월 + K00~K08 diagnosis + covered treatment = Potential eligibility to claim reimbursement of your co-pay. However, the final decision follows your policy and the insurer’s review.

This guide provides general information based on standard indemnity insurance terms and publicly available insurance/NHIS information. Actual coverage depends on enrollment timing, product, riders, and your individual policy so please confirm with your insurer before filing a claim. (Written: 2026년 6월, Seoul BD Dental)

Most commonly missed case: Wisdom Tooth

Impacted Wisdom Tooth extraction is a covered treatment, and your out-of-pocket copay can reach the tens of thousands of won; many people don’t file claims because they think "dental isn’t reimbursable under indemnity plans." If you enrolled in an indemnity plan in or after October 2009, keep your receipt and itemized statement and consider filing a claim.

05Check your indemnity health insurance generation (1~5 generations)

Indemnity health insurance is divided into "generations" by enrollment date, with different copay rates and claim conditions. Confirming which generation you are inis the starting point for a claim.

GenerationSales periodKey points for dental coverage
1st generation~2009.9Pre-standardization. Most dental treatments excluded from coverage (some rider exceptions)
2nd generation (standardized)2009.10~2017.3Begins covering the patient copay for NHIS-covered dental care
3rd generation2017.4~2021.6Covers NHIS copays; copay structure changed
4th generation2021.7~Covered and non-covered split; premiums vary based on non-covered usage
5th generationLaunches 2026.5Latest revised product. Check each policy’s coverage structure

How to check your generation (1 min)

  1. 내보험찾아줌 (cont.insure.or.kr) or confirm your enrollment date in your insurer’s app
  2. Compare your enrollment date with the table above to identify your generation
  3. Call your insurer’s call center and ask directly whether your plan covers the patient copay for NHIS-covered dental treatment

06Claim documents and process — what to obtain from the dental clinic

If you prepare the documents properly, submitting the claim via your insurer’s app takes about 5 minutes.

Basic documents

  • Treatment fee invoice/receipt — showing covered by Korean NHIS vs non-covered (out-of-pocket)
  • Itemized statement of treatment charges — lets you check, by item, which treatments are covered by Korean NHIS
  • Medical certificate or outpatient visit confirmation — if required by your insurer (with diagnosis code)

Simplified small-amount claims

If your outpatient medical expense falls within the small-amount range (thresholds vary by insurer/policy, commonly 3만~10만원), many insurers allow a simplified claim without a diagnosis certificate—using just the receipt and prescription. Check your insurer’s app or call center for the exact criteria.

How to file a claim

  1. At the front desk "Please give me the documents for a reimbursement claim" request (invoice + itemized statement)
  2. Go to the claim menu in your insurer’s app/website
  3. Take photos of the documents and upload → submit
  4. Payment after insurer review (usually within a few business days)

07Pre-claim self-checklist

Checking the four items below before you claim can save you an unnecessary trip.

  1. Enrollment date — Is my indemnity plan enrolled in 2009년 10월 or later (2세대 이상)?
  2. Diagnosis code — Is the treatment diagnosed as a dental condition (K00~K08)? (Check on the itemized statement/medical certificate)
  3. NHIS coverage status — Does the receipt mark the treatment as "급여" (covered by Korean NHIS)?
  4. Confirm with your insurer — Even if the three items above fit, final coverage depends on policy terms and review—did you check with your insurer in advance?

08Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q.Can I claim reimbursement for dental treatment too?

Some treatments, yes. For policies enrolled 2009년 10월 이후 standardized indemnity plan policyholders who received care under a dental diagnosis (K00~K08) the patient co-pay for covered treatments may be eligible to claim. Typical examples include Wisdom Tooth extraction, Root Canal Treatment, and medically necessary Scaling (Dental Cleaning). On the other hand, Dental Implant, Composite Resin, Crown, and Orthodontics are non-covered and generally not eligible.

Q.Are Dental Implants not reimbursable at all?

Under the standard policy, Dental Implants are non-covered, so claims are generally not allowed. Instead, 만 65세 이상이라면 coverage may be available through Korean NHIS Up to 2 in a lifetime; 30% patient co-payDental implants can be covered (bone graft (GBR) is non-covered and billed separately). Tooth damage from an accident may be covered under an accident rider, so please check with your insurer.

Q.Is Scaling (Dental Cleaning) covered or not?

It depends on the purpose. For gingivitis or periodontitis For treatment Scaling (covered by Korean NHIS) is eligible for claim review, but the once-per-year NHIS-covered For prevention Scaling is not for disease treatment, so it is not eligible under indemnity insurance. Check whether the itemized statement includes a periodontal disease diagnosis code.

Q.Can the Crown placed after a Root Canal Treatment be claimed together?

No, they must be handled separately. Root Canal Treatment itself is covered by Korean NHIS so your co-pay can be reviewed for reimbursement, but the Crown is non-covered (out-of-pocket) and is generally excluded. Even for treatment on the same tooth, covered and non-covered items are listed separately on the receipt, so be sure to check the itemized statement.

Q.I have an older policy (before 2009). Is there any way to claim?

In principle, it’s difficult, but some first-generation plans include dental coverage as a rider. Check your policy terms (certificate) from when you enrolled, or call your insurer’s call center and directly ask, "Are covered dental treatments included?" That’s the most accurate way.

Q.How do I request the documents from the dental clinic?

At the front desk "For an indemnity claim, please give me the medical receipt and the itemized statement."—that’s all you need to say. If your insurer requires a medical certificate, we can also issue one with the diagnosis code. At Seoul BD Dental, all of these can be issued the same day.
