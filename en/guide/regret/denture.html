How to keep them from ending up in a drawer

Half of well-made dentures end up in a drawer. Not because dentures are bad, but because no one explained the adjustment period and follow-up adjustments in advance. We’ll share the honest realities of dentures before you have them made.

Written by the Seoul BD Dental Medical Team As of June 2026 Approx. 7-minute read

30-second key summary

Most denture regrets are: ① Not knowing the adjustment period (several weeks to months) and the need for fine-tuning, then giving up after calling it an "ill-fitting denture," ② Letting a denture loosen as the jawbone shrinks and leaving it that way, creating a vicious cycle, ③ Learning about the Implant Overdenture option too lateThat’s most of it. If you confirm the number of post-delivery adjustment visits in advance, get a yearly checkup, and have the Implant Overdenture option compared and explained before deciding, regret drops dramatically. For those 65 and older, it is covered by the Korean NHIS once every 7 years.

01The 4 real reasons people regret dentures

A denture isn’t a prosthesis you simply 'fit' but a prosthesis you 'adapt to'If you begin without this mindset, regret follows; with it, you’ll adapt.

Regret 1“It was uncomfortable, so I put it in a drawer” — Giving up on adapting
Why it happensDentures are not comfortable the moment you put them in. At first you may feel a foreign feeling, looseness, speech difficulty, and pain; it usually takes several weeks to months to adapt, with multiple adjustment visits. If you don’t know this, it’s easy to assume it’s a “badly made denture” and give up.
PreventionBefore fabrication how many adjustment visits are includedConfirm this, and don’t endure sore spots—get them adjusted as they come up. Pointing out painful areas is the shortest path to adapting. Never grind or trim the denture yourself.
Regret 2Leaving a loose denture alone — the start of a vicious cycle
Why it happensThe jawbone under the gums resorbs little by little each year, so the denture gets looser over time. If you keep wearing a loose denture, it can cause gum sores and inflammation and accelerate bone loss—a vicious cycle. The most common pattern is “toughing it out because it’s the one I’ve been using.”
PreventionGet a checkup once a yearand, if needed, have the inside refitted with a reline (relining). A denture typically lasts 5~7 years—know in advance that it isn’t permanent.
Regret 3“I didn’t know about an Implant Overdenture”
Why it happensA conventional denture provides only about 20~30% of natural chewing force. An Implant Overdenture anchored on 2~4 Dental Implants greatly improves chewing power and retention, but many people only learn about this option after their denture is already made and regret it.
PreventionBefore deciding on a denture get a comparison and explanation of the costs and pros/cons of the Implant Overdenture optionPlease ask for this. The looser and more troublesome a full lower denture is, the greater the improvement in satisfaction with an Implant Overdenture.
Regret 4Speech and eating changes — “I don’t feel like meeting people anymore”
Why it happensIf you aren’t told in advance about initial speech difficulties and eating limits (sticky foods, hard foods), it can even lead to avoiding social life. A functional issue can become an emotional one.
PreventionFor the first 1~2 months start with soft foods and progress gradually, and improve your speech faster by practicing reading aloud. Knowing it’s a temporary process makes it much easier to get through.

02Denture side effects — frequency and what to do

Most denture side effects come from “continuing to wear an ill‑fitting denture.” Regular checkups are the best prevention.

Side effectFrequency/patternWhat to do
Gum pain/pressure ulcersCommon — initial adjustment periodResolved with adjustments. If you “tough it out,” ulcers worsen—get an adjustment right away.
Loosening (reduced retention)Inevitable over timeRelining and remake. Determine the timing with a once-a-year check-up.
Stomatitis · denture stomatitisCommon when hygiene is poorClean daily, remove and store while sleeping, and give your gums time to rest
Accelerated jawbone resorptionWith long-term use of an ill-fitting dentureRegular check-ups and adjustments are the only prevention
Metal clasps (hooks) put stress on the teethOften with partial denturesCheck the abutment (anchor) teeth regularly; come in immediately if any are loose
Dulled taste and temperature sensationCommon with a complete upper dentureA structural limitation. If you can’t tolerate it, consider an Implant Overdenture
The frequencies and patterns summarized here are general trends and may vary by your oral condition and overall health. Please confirm your exact risk through a consultation with our clinicians.

03If this sounds like you, please think twice

To be frank, dentures aren’t the right answer for everyone. If any of the following apply, discuss alternatives with the clinical team before rushing into treatment.

  • If even a few teeth can be saved — A partial denture using the remaining teeth or a plan combined with Dental Implants may be possible. Before extracting and moving to a complete denture, confirm that it’s truly the best option.
  • Not prepared to go through an adjustment period — Dentures aren’t comfortable from day one. If you can’t accept the “getting‑used‑to” process, consider a plan focused on Dental Implants first.
  • A diet that requires strong chewing power — and you can’t give that up — Conventional dentures provide about 20~30% of the chewing power of natural teeth. Compare alternatives such as an Implant Overdenture or full-arch Dental Implants.
  • Unable to attend regular check-ups — Dentures aren’t a “make it and you’re done” solution; they need yearly adjustments. Without check-ups, you’ll lose jawbone faster.

04Pre-treatment checklist — Checking these will greatly lower your chance of regret

Any dental clinic is fine. If a clinic can clearly answer the points below, you can feel comfortable getting treatment there.

  • Alternatives such as an Implant Overdenture were compared and explainedI was given this explanation
  • After fabrication the number of visits including adjustment appointmentswas confirmed
  • I understand the adjustment period (several weeks to months) and initial discomfort
  • I was informed of the denture lifespan (5~7 years) and the relining/remake cycle
  • I confirmed whether Korean NHIS coverage applies at age 65+ (once every 7 years)
  • I was instructed on daily cleaning and how to store the denture during sleep

05Frequently Asked Questions

Q.My denture is very uncomfortable—was it made incorrectly?
It’s too early to conclude. Dentures require an adjustment period of several weeks to months and multiple adjustments. It’s normal to point out specific sore spots and have them adjusted. However, if several adjustments bring no improvement at all, a fit reassessment or a remake consultation may be needed.
Q.How long do dentures last?
Usually 5~7 years. While the denture itself can wear down, a bigger reason is that the jawbone resorbs and the fit changes. Getting a checkup once a year and doing a reline (inner surface adjustment) when needed can extend its lifespan. For those aged 65 and over, a new denture is covered by Korean NHIS once every 7 years.
Q.How is an Implant Overdenture different?
We place 2~4 Dental Implants so the denture snaps into place. Retention and chewing power are much better than with a conventional denture, and satisfaction is especially higher for very loose lower full dentures. It does cost more, but if you’ve struggled with regular dentures, it’s well worth considering.
Q.Can I sleep with my denture in?
We don’t recommend it. Your gums also need time to rest, and sleeping with dentures in increases the risk of denture stomatitis and jawbone resorption. At bedtime, remove it, clean it, and store it in water or a cleansing solution.
Q.Are dentures covered by Korean NHIS?
Yes. For those aged 65 and over, both complete and partial dentures are covered by Korean NHIS with a 30% patient copay, and it applies once every 7 years. Up to 6 adjustments within 3 months after fabrication are also covered, so please take full advantage.
