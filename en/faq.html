Cost/Payment

Starts from 0:55

Fair, transparent pricing standards

"How much does a Dental Implant cost?" — The Head Director personally explains the real factors that determine pricing.

Dental Implant price, materials, and aftercare—organized in one place

At Seoul BD Dental, your first visit includes a BDX precision exam.

  • Panoramic X-ray + CT + intraoral scanner exam
  • Specialist consultation and Treatment plan guidance
  • Integrated exam report provided

We do not pressure you into treatment after the exam; please take your time to decide. For each treatment’s fees, Non-covered (out-of-pocket) fee schedule & pricing is available to review in advance.

At Seoul BD Dental, Dental Implants cost 800,000–1,600,000 KRW per tooth (approx. $580–$1,170).

  • Osstem CA (Korean-made, standard) - 800,000 KRW (approx. $580)
  • Osstem SOI (Korean-made, premium) - 1,000,000 KRW (approx. $730)
  • Straumann BLX (Genuine Swiss) - 1,600,000 KRW (approx. $1,170)

If the gum bone is insufficient, a Bone Graft (GBR) may be added (300,000–1,000,000 KRW; approx. $220–$730). For patients aged 65 and older, up to two implants in a lifetime are covered by Korean NHIS (30% patient co-pay).

See all Dental Implant prices →

Invisalign (clear aligners) costs 3,000,000–7,000,000 KRW (approx. $2,190–$5,110). (Express 3,000,000 KRW (approx. $2,190) ~ Comprehensive 7,000,000 KRW (approx. $5,110))

  • Clippy-C (self-ligating brackets) - 5,000,000 KRW (approx. $3,650)
  • Clarity Ultra (ceramic) - 5,500,000 KRW (approx. $4,015)

The program is determined by treatment scope (partial/full) and the amount of tooth movement, and a 3D simulation lets you preview results before treatment.

See all Orthodontic prices →

Laminate Veneer costs vary based on the following:

  • Tooth condition - Color, shape, and alignment of your existing teeth
  • Scope of treatment - Number of teeth to be treated (usually 4~10 teeth)
  • Materials - Ceramic Types and fabrication methods

At Seoul BD Dental Glownate Laminate Veneer (Premium) is 800,000 KRW per tooth (approx. $580, single flat price)The exact cost after a comprehensive exam and consultation will be explained.

"Does a Dental Implant procedure hurt a lot?" — We answer candidly about pain levels and individual differences.

Post-implant pain and care Q&A

Invisalign The treatment duration varies by your dental condition:

  • Partial treatment (Invisalign Go) - approx. 3–6 months
  • Comprehensive treatment - approx. 12–18 months
  • Complex cases - approx. 18–24 months

You can preview expected results and duration before treatment with 3D simulation.

Seoul BD Dental minimizes pain with digital, guided surgery.

  • During the procedure - No pain thanks to anesthesia
  • After the procedure - Discomfort similar to a routine extraction (2–3 days)
  • Recovery period - Pain medication and attentive aftercare provided

If you are sensitive to pain, please tell us during your consultation; we can guide you on additional sedation options.

Seoul BD Dental’s Glownateis based on minimal tooth reduction.

  • Conventional Laminate Veneer - 0.5–1 mm reduction on the front surface of the tooth
  • Glownate - Minimal reduction 0.3–0.5 mm
  • No-prep Laminate Veneer - Depending on the case, possible with no reduction

We evaluate your teeth and suggest the option that achieves excellent results with minimal reduction.

It depends on how advanced it is.

  • Early cavity - Monitor and treat if needed ( to prevent cavities can manage it)
  • Moderate cavity - Composite Resin or Inlay recommended
  • Severe cavity - Root Canal Treatment after Crown needed

Seoul BD Dental follows a no-overtreatment philosophy, only the treatments you truly need we recommend.

With proper care, they can be used long-term.

  • Implant body (fixture) - Success rate over 95% at 10+ years
  • Top restoration (crown/abutment) - May need replacement after 10–15 years

How to extend the lifespan:

  • Regular checkups (6 months–1 year)
  • Proper oral hygiene
  • Quit smoking, Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) treatment

They typically last about 1~3 years.

  • Professional whitening (in-office) - Immediate results, lasts 1~2 years
  • At-home whitening (home) - Gradual results, can be maintained regularly

Tips to maintain your results:

  • Cut down on stain-causing foods like coffee, wine, and curry
  • Quit smoking
  • Touch-up whitening every 6 months

Aftercare

Skip to 5:30

Must-follow aftercare guidelines

"How do I care for my teeth after treatment?" — Three essential rules to help your Dental Implant last.

Dental Implant prices, materials, and maintenance—at a glance

Seoul BD Dental operates a structured aftercare program.

  • Dental Implant - 3 months, 6 months, and 1 year after placement Regular checkups
  • Orthodontics - Retainer Check every 3–6 months while wearing it
  • Laminate Veneer - Check condition every 6 months–1 year
  • General Dentistry - Routine checkup recommended every 6 months

We will send a reminder via KakaoTalk or text when it’s time for your checkup.

Please visit the clinic as soon as possible.

  • If detached - Keep the veneer clean and bring it with you (re-bonding may be possible)
  • If broken - Remake required (no-charge or discounted within the warranty period)

Seoul BD Dental Glownate includes complimentary service within the warranty period.

In an emergency, call us and we will help arrange a prompt appointment.

You may eat on the day of surgery, but caution is needed.

  • Same day–3 days - Soft foods (porridge, soup, tofu, etc.)
  • 1 week - Soft solids on the opposite side
  • 2 weeks–1 month - Gradually return to a normal diet
  • After the final restoration - Normal eating is possible

Please avoid chewing on the surgical site.

Long-term wear is recommended to maintain your orthodontic results.

  • First 6 months after orthodontic treatment - At least 22 hours a day (except while eating or brushing)
  • 6 months–1 year - At least 12 hours a day (during sleep)
  • After 1 year - Recommended to wear during sleep (lifelong)

Without a retainer, your teeth may move back toward their original position.
